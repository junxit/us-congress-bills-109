# H.R. 3639

> To establish minimum standards relating to a factor for life insurance eligibility and rates, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Life Insurance Fairness for Travelers Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Amendments to TRIA

The Terrorism Risk Insurance Act of 2002 (Public Law 107–297; 116 Stat. 2322) is amended by adding at the end the following new title:

## § 3. Timing of regulations

The Secretary of the Treasury shall issue the regulations required by section 406 of the Terrorism Risk Insurance Act of 2002, as added by section 2 of this Act, not later than the expiration of the 6-month period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act.
