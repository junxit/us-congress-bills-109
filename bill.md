# H.R. 3014

> To amend the Act of August 9, 1955, regarding leasing of the Moses Allotments.

## § 1. Amendment related to Moses Allotments

The first section of the Act of August 9, 1955 (69 Stat. 539; 25 U.S.C. 415), is amended by striking “and the lands comprising the Moses Allotment Numbered 10, Chelan County, Washington,” and inserting the following: “and the lands comprising the Moses Allotment Numbered 8 and the Moses Allotment Numbered 10, Chelan County, Washington”.
