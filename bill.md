# H.R. 3033

> To extend the temporary reduction in duty on certain educational devices.

## § 1. Certain educational devices

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.85.43 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain educational devices) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “1.67%” and inserting “Free”; and
  - **(2)** in the effective period column, by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2008”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
