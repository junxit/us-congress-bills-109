# S. 3941

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to fully allow students to live in units eligible for the low-income housing credit, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Clarification of student housing eligible for low-income housing credit

- **(a)** *In general* Section 42(i)(3)(D) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to certain students not to disqualify unit) is amended by striking all after clause (i) and inserting the following:
  > - **(ii)** entirely by full-time students if such students are—
  >   - **(I)** single parents and their children,
  >   - **(II)** married and file a joint return, or
  >   - **(III)** adult students fulfilling the requirements for a secondary school education or secondary education equivalency diploma.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.
