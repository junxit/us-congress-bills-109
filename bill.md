# H.R. 3016

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on carbamic acid.

## § 1. Carbamic Acid

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.33.61 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to ((3-((Dimethylamino)carbonyl)-2-pyridinyl)sulfonyl) carbamic acid, phenyl ester) is amended by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2009”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
