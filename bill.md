# S. 1613

> To amend the Livestock Mandatory Reporting Act of 1999 to extend the termination date for mandatory price reporting.

## § 1. Extension of mandatory price reporting

Section 942 of the Livestock Mandatory Reporting Act of 1999 (Public Law 106–78; 7 U.S.C. 1635 note) is amended by striking “September 30, 2005” and inserting “September 30, 2006”.
