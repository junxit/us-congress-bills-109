# H.R. 1588

> To improve programs for the identification and treatment of post-deployment mental health conditions, including post-traumatic stress disorder, in veterans and members of the Armed Forces, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Comprehensive Assistance for Veterans Exposed to Traumatic Stressors Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Definition

In this Act, the term PTSD means post-traumatic stress disorder.
