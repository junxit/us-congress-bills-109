# S. 2218

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain sawing machines.

## § 1. Certain sawing machines

- **(a)** *In General* Heading 9902.84.91 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain manufacturing equipment) is amended by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2009”.
- **(b)** *Effective Date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
