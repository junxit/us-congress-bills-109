# H.R. 2383

> To redesignate the facility of the Bureau of Reclamation located at 19550 Kelso Road in Byron, California, as the “C.W. ‘Bill’ Jones Pumping Plant”.

## § 1. Redesignation of facility

The facility of the Bureau of Reclamation located at 15990 Kelso Road in Byron, California, and known as the Tracy Pumping Plant, shall be known and designated as the “C.W. “Bill” Jones Pumping Plant”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “C.W. “Bill” Jones Pumping Plant”.
