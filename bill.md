# H.R. 2473

> To amend the Tariff Act of 1930 relating to determining the all-others rate in antidumping cases.

## § 1. Determination of all-others rate in antidumping cases

Subparagraphs (A) and (B) of section 735(c)(5) of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1673d(c)(5)) are each amended by striking “entirely”.
