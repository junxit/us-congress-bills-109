# H.R. 1028

> To amend the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 with respect to enforcement provisions.

## § 1. Penalties

Section 17 of the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 (29 U.S.C. 666) is amended by inserting the following:

> - **(m)** The Secretary shall not use “other than serious” citations as a basis for issuing subsequent, repeat or willful citations.
