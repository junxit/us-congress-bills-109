# H.J. RES. 84

> Proposing an amendment to the Constitution of the United States to limit the power of Federal courts to force a State or local government to levy or increase taxes.

## § 1. Short title

This Joint Resolution may be cited as the “No Taxation Without Representation Amendment”.

## § 2. Proposal of Amendment to the Constitution of the United States
