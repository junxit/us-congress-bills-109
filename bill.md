# H. RES. 1003

> Providing for the adoption of the resolution (H. Res. 1000) providing for earmarking reform in the House of Representatives.

That upon adoption of this resolution, House Resolution 1000, amended by the amendment in the nature of a substitute recommended by the Committee on Rules now printed in the resolution, is hereby adopted.
