# S. 1340

> To amend the Pittman-Robertson Wildlife Restoration Act to extend the date after which surplus funds in the wildlife restoration fund become available for apportionment.

## § 1. Availability of surplus funds in wildlife restoration fund

Section 3(b)(2)(C) of the Pittman-Robertson Wildlife Restoration Act (16 U.S.C. 669b(b)(2)(C) is amended by striking “2006” and inserting “2016”.
