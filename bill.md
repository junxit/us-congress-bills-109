# H. RES. 1101

> Waiving points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 5682) to exempt from certain requirements of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 a proposed nuclear agreement for cooperation with India.

That upon the adoption of this resolution it shall be in order to consider the conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 5682) to exempt from certain requirements of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 a proposed nuclear agreement for cooperation with India. All points of order against the conference report and against its consideration are waived. The conference report shall be considered as read.
