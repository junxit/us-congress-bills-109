# S. CON. RES. 77

> To provide for a joint session of Congress to receive a message from the President on the state of the Union.

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, January 31, 2006, at 9 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.
