# H.R. 3768

> To provide emergency tax relief for persons affected by Hurricane Katrina.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Katrina Emergency Tax Relief Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Designation as emergency requirement

Any provision of this Act causing an effect on receipts, budget authority, or outlays is designated as an emergency requirement pursuant to section 402 of H. Con. Res. 95 (109th Congress).
