# H.R. 1947

> To provide for the reliquidation of certain entries.

## § 1. Certain entries of soundspa clock radios

- **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding section 514 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1514) or any other provision of law, the Bureau of Customs and Border Protection shall, not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act—
  - **(1)** reliquidate each entry described in subsection (c) containing any merchandise which, on the date of original liquidation, was classified under subheading 8527.19.50 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States; and
  - **(2)** make such reliquidation at the rate of duty that would have been applicable to such merchandise if the merchandise had been liquidated under subheading 8527.19.10 of such Schedule on the date of entry of the merchandise.
- **(b)** *Refund of amounts owed* Any amounts owed by the United States under subsection (a) shall be refunded with interest.
