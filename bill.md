# H.R. 6051

> To designate the Federal building located at 2 South Main Street in Akron, Ohio, as the “John F. Seiberling Federal Building”.

## § 1. Designation

The Federal building located at 2 South Main Street in Akron, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the “John F. Seiberling Federal Building”.

## § 2. References

Any references in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “John F. Seiberling Federal Building”.
