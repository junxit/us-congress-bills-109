# H.R. 5238

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to eliminate adjustments in Medicare payments for imaging services made by section 5102 of the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005.

## § 1. Elimination of medicare payment adjustments for imaging services

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1848 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–4), as amended by section 5102 of the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005 (Public Law 109–171), is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (b), by striking paragraph (4); and
  - **(2)** in subsection (c)(2)(B)—
    - **(A)** clause (ii)(II), by striking “clauses (iv) and (v)” and inserting “clause (iv)”;
    - **(B)** in the heading of clause (iv), by striking “of certain additional expenditures”; and
    - **(C)** by striking clause (v).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall be effective with respect to fee schedules established beginning with 2007 and for services furnished on or after January 1, 2007.
