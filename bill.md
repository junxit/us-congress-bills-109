# S. 1331

> To amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 to change the date of implementation of country of origin labeling to January 30, 2006.

## § 1. Applicability of country of origin labeling

Section 285 of the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 (7 U.S.C. 1638d) is amended by striking “September 30, 2006” and inserting “January 30, 2006”.
