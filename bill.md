# S. 1159

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to permanently extend the subpart F exemption for active financing.

## § 1. Permanent extension of subpart F exemption for active financing

- **(a)** *In general* Section 954(h)(9) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “and before January 1, 2007,”.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendments* Section 953(e)(10) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “and before January 1, 2007,”, and
  - **(2)** by striking the second sentence thereof.
