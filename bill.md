# S. 1973

> To provide an immediate Federal income tax rebate to help taxpayers with higher fuel costs, to express the sense of the Senate regarding full funding of LIHEAP, and to provide consumer protections against fuel price gouging, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Energy Tax Rebate Act of 2005”.
