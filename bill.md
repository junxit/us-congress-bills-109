# H.R. 4764

> To amend section 1368 of title 18, United States Code, to include rescue dogs in its protection.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Canine Volunteer Protection Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Definition of “police animal” for the purposes of section 1368 of title 18, United States Code

Section 1368(b) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “or commissioned” after “employed”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “or participating in search and rescue missions” after “criminal offenders”.
