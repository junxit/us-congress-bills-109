# H.R. 5546

> To designate the Federal courthouse to be constructed in Greenville, South Carolina, as the “Carroll A. Campbell, Jr. Federal Courthouse”.

## § 1. Carroll A. Campbell, Jr. Federal Courthouse

- **(a)** *Designation* The Federal courthouse to be constructed in Greenville, South Carolina, building number SC0017ZZ, shall be known and designated as the “Carroll A. Campbell, Jr. Federal Courthouse”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal courthouse referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Carroll A. Campbell, Jr. Federal Courthouse.
