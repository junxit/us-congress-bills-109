# S. RES. 242

> To express the sense of the Senate that the President should appoint an individual to oversee Federal funds for the Hurricane Katrina recovery, and for other purposes.

It is the sense of the Senate that the President, in order to efficiently coordinate and monitor spending, avoid duplication, and eliminate waste, fraud, and abuse, shall appoint an individual to oversee all Federal work and the obligation of all federally appropriated funds for the purpose of Hurricane Katrina recovery, rehabilitation, and reconstruction.
