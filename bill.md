# S. 2467

> To enhance and improve the trade relations of the United States by strengthening United States trade enforcement efforts and encouraging United States trading partners to adhere to the rules and norms of international trade, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “United States Trade Enhancement Act of 2006”.
