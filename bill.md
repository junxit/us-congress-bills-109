# H.R. 4457

> To identify certain roads in the vicinity of Fort Belvoir, Virginia, as defense access roads for purposes of the Defense Access Road Program.

## § 1. Identification of defense access roads, Fort Belvoir, Virginia

- **(a)** *Additional defense access roads* For purposes of the Defense Access Road Program, the roads described in subsection (b) in the vicinity of Fort Belvoir, Virginia, are hereby certified to the Secretary of Defense and the Secretary of Transportation as important to the national defense pursuant to section 210 of title 23, United States Code.
- **(b)** *Covered roads* The certification made by subsection (a) applies to those portions of the following roads in the vicinity of Fort Belvoir:
  - **(1)** Interstate highway I-95.
  - **(2)** Virginia Route 7100 (Fairfax County Parkway).
  - **(3)** U.S. Route 1 (Richmond Highway).
  - **(4)** Virginia Route 611(Telegraph Road).
