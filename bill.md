# S. 2657

> To extend the Iran and Libya Sanctions Act of 1996

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Iran Sanctions Extension Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Extension of authorities regarding sanctions on Iran

Section 13(b) of the Iran and Libya Sanctions Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–172; 50 U.S.C. 1701 note) is amended by striking “10 years” and inserting “15 years”.
