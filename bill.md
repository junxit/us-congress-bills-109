# H.R. 3203

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to expand and extend the eligibility of Hispanic-serving institutions for assistance under title V of that Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Hispanic-Serving Institution Assistance Improvement Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Extension of Eligibility of Hispanic-Serving Institutions: Elimination of wait-out period

Section 504(a) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1101c(a)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(a)** *Award Period* The Secretary may award a grant to a Hispanic-serving institution under this title for 5 years.
