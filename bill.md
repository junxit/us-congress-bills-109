# H.R. 3979

> To suspend temporarily the provisions of title 5, United States Code, relating to the mandatory separation of members of the Capitol Police by reason of age or years of service.

## § 1. Temporary suspension of mandatory separation provisions

No member of the Capitol Police shall, during the 12-month period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act, be subject to mandatory separation under either section 8335(c) or section 8425(c) of title 5, United States Code.
