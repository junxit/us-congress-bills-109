# H.R. 2460

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain yarn of carded Kashmir (cashmere).

## § 1. Certain yarn of carded Kashmir (cashmere)

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.03.02 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain yarn of carded Kashmir (cashmere)) is amended as follows:
  - **(1)** By striking “of 6 run or finer (equivalent to 19.35 metric yarn system)” and inserting “of 19.35 metric yarn count or finer”.
  - **(2)** By striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2008”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
