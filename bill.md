# H. RES. 871

> Recognizing Sam Hornish, Jr. for winning the 90th running of the Indianapolis 500.

## Preamble

- Whereas Sam Hornish, Jr. was born in Bryan, Ohio, was raised in Defiance, Ohio, and currently resides in Napoleon, Ohio, all of which are in Ohio’s 5th Congressional District;
- Whereas Sam Hornish, Jr. competes in the Indy Racing League with Team Penske and has twice won the championship of that league; and
- Whereas Sam Hornish, Jr. won the 90th running of the Indianapolis 500 on May 28, 2006: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives recognizes Sam Hornish, Jr. for his victory in the Indianapolis 500.
