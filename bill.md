# H.R. 4336

> To designate the visitor center and other related facilities at the U.S.S. Arizona Memorial in Hawaii as the “Pearl Harbor Memorial Site”.

## § 1. Designation of Pearl Harbor Memorial Site Visitor Center and other related facilities

- **(a)** *Designation* The visitor center, museum, bookstore, exhibits, and other related facilities located on the shore at the U.S.S. Arizona Memorial in Hawaii, are hereby designated as the “Pearl Harbor Memorial Site”.
- **(b)** *Reference* Any reference to the visitor center, museum, bookstore, exhibits, and other related facilities located on the shore at the U.S.S. Arizona Memorial in Hawaii, in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the “Pearl Harbor Memorial Site”.
