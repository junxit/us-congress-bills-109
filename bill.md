# H.R. 3119

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on carfentazone ethyl.

## § 1. Carfentazone ethyl

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9901.01.54 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to carfentazone ethyl) is amended by striking ‘‘12/31/2006’’ and inserting ‘‘12/31/2009’’.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
