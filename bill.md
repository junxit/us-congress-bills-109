# H.R. 1394

> To amend the Small Business Act to clarify that the Administrator of the Small Business Administration is authorized to make economic injury disaster loans in response to disasters caused by drought.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Administration Drought Assistance Act”.

## § 2. Economic injury loans for disasters caused by drought

Section 7(b)(2) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 636(b)(2)) is amended by inserting “(including a drought)” after “in an area affected by a disaster”.
