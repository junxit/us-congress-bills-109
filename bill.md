# H.R. 5618

> To extend the patent term for the badge of the American Legion Women’s Auxiliary, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Patent Term Extension for the Badge of the American Legion Women’s Auxiliary

The term of a certain design patent numbered 55,398 (for the badge of the American Legion Women's Auxiliary) is renewed and extended for a period of 14 years beginning on the date of enactment of this Act, with all the rights and privileges pertaining to such patent.
