# H. RES. 916

> Impeaching Manuel L. Real, judge of the United States District Court for the Central District of California, for high crimes and misdemeanors.

That Manuel L. Real, judge of the United States District Court for the Central District of California, is impeached for high crimes and misdemeanors.
