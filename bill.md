# H.R. 1804

> To prescribe the oath of renunciation and allegiance for purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act.

## § 1. Oath of renunciation and allegiance

Section 337(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1448(a)) is amended by inserting after “the child is unable to understand its meaning.” the following: “The oath referred to in this section shall be the oath provided for in paragraph (a) or (b) of section 337.1 of title 8, Code of Federal Regulations, as in effect on April 1, 2005.”.
