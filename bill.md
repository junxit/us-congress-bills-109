## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Central map* The term “Central Map” means the map entitled “Central Washington County, Utah” and dated _____.
- **(2)** *County* The term County means Washington County, Utah.
- **(3)** *Eastern map* The term “Eastern Map” means the map entitled “Eastern Washington County, Utah” and dated _____.
- **(4)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior.
- **(5)** *State* The term State means the State of Utah.
- **(6)** *Western map* The term “Western Map” means the map entitled “Western Washington County, Utah” and dated _____.
