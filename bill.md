# H.R. 3149

> To withdraw the Los Padres National Forest in California from location, entry, and patent under mining laws, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Withdrawal of Los Padres National Forest, California, from mining laws

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Los Padres National Forest Conservation Act”.
- **(b)** *Withdrawal* Subject to valid existing rights, the Los Padres National Forest in California is hereby withdrawn from—
  - **(1)** all forms of entry, appropriation, and disposal under the public land laws;
  - **(2)** location, entry, and patent under the mining laws; and
  - **(3)** operation of the mineral leasing, mineral materials, and geothermal leasing laws.
