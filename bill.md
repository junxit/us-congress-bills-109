# S. 1892

> To amend Public Law 107–153 to modify a certain date.

## § 1. Settlement of tribal claims

Section 1(a) of Public Law 107–153 (25 U.S.C. 4044 note; 116 Stat. 79) is amended by striking “December 31, 1999” and inserting “December 31, 2005”.
