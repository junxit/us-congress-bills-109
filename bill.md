# H.R. 5446

> To direct the Administrator of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration to report to the Congress on the effects of Hurricanes Katrina, Rita, and Wilma on the fisheries and fish habitat of the United States.

## § 1. Report on effects of hurricanes

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Administrator of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration shall report to the Congress on the effects of Hurricanes Katrina, Rita, and Wilma on the fisheries and fish habitat of the United States.
- **(b)** *Contents* The report shall include a description of the effects of such hurricanes on—
  - **(1)** commercial and recreational fisheries;
  - **(2)** owners and operators of shrimp fishing vessels; and
  - **(3)** the oyster industry.
