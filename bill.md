# H.R. 3110

> To amend the Endangered Species Act of 1973 to provide for treatment of distinct population segments of the Eastern oyster as separate species.

## § 1. Treatment of distinct population segments of Eastern oyster as separate species

Section 3(16) of the Endangered Species Act of 1973 (16 U.S.C. 1532(16)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and any” and inserting “, any”; and
- **(2)** by inserting before the period the following: “, and any distinct population segment of the Eastern oyster (Crassostrea virginica) which interbreeds when mature”.
