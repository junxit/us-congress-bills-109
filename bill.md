# H.J. RES. 93

> Disapproving the issuance of letters of offer with respect to certain proposed sales of defense articles and defense services to Pakistan.

That the issuance of a letter of offer with respect to any of the following proposed sales to Pakistan (described in the certifications transmitted to the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the Chairman of the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate pursuant to section 36(b) of the Arms Export Control Act on June 28, 2006) is hereby prohibited:

- **(1)** The proposed sale of defense articles and defense services (Transmittal Number 06–09).
- **(2)** The proposed sale of defense articles and defense services (Transmittal Number 06–11).
- **(3)** The proposed sale of defense articles and defense services (Transmittal Number 06–34).
