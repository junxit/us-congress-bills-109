# H.R. 5461

> To authorize the Secretary of the Army to carry out water resources projects and activities for the coastal area of Louisiana, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Meeting Authorization Requirements for the Coast Act of 2006”.
- **(b)** *Table of contents*

## § 2. Definition of Secretary

In this Act, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Army.
