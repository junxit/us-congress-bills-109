# H.R. 3090

> To suspend temporarily the duty on Pyromellitic Dianhydride (PMDA).

## § 1. Temporary suspension of duty

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.05.14 of subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, relating to Pyromellitic Dianhydride (CAS # 89–32–7) is amended by striking 12/31/2006 and inserting 12/31/2009.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
