# H.R. 3065

> To extend the aviation war risk insurance program for 3 years.

## § 1. Extension of airline war risk policies and terrorism coverage

- **(a)** *Extension of policies* Section 44302(f)(1) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by striking “August 31, 2005, and shall extend through December 31, 2005,” in paragraph (1) and inserting “August 31, 2008, and shall extend through December 31, 2008,”.
- **(b)** *Extension of terrorism coverage* Section 44303(b) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by striking “December 31, 2005,” and inserting “December 31, 2008,”.
