# H. RES. 1024

> Directing the Attorney General to provide certain information to the House of Representatives relating to Maher Arar.

That the Attorney General is directed to provide to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, all documents and records in the possession of the Attorney General relating to Maher Arar.
