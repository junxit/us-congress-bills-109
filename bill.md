# H.R. 2809

> To temporarily suspend the duty on Carfentrazone.

## § 1. Carfentrazone

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.01.54 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Carfentrazone) is amended by striking “4.9%” and inserting “free” and by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2009”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
