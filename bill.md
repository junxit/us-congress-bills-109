# H.R. 3188

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to provide protection for immigrant victims of violence.

## § 1. Short title

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as “Immigrant Victims of Violence Protection Act of 2005”.
- **(b)** *Table of contents* The table of contents of this Act is as follows:
- **(c)** *References to VAWA–2000* In this Act, the term VAWA–2000 means the Violence Against Women Act of 2000 (division B of Public Law 106–386).
- **(d)** *Regulations* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Attorney General, the Secretary of Homeland Security, and Secretary of State shall promulgate regulations to implement the provisions contained in the Battered Immigrant Women Protection Act of 2000 (title V of VAWA–2000) and the amendments made by (and the provisions of) this Act.
