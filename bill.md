# H. RES. 1064

That upon adoption of this resolution it shall be in order to consider the conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 4954) to improve maritime and cargo security through enhanced layered defenses, and for other purposes. All points of order against the conference report and against its consideration are waived. The conference report shall be considered as read.
