# H.R. 4528

> To designate the Federal building and United States courthouse located at 111 North Adams Street in Tallahassee, Florida, as the “William Stafford United States Courthouse”.

## § 1. Designation

The United States courthouse located at 111 North Adams Street in Tallahassee, Florida, shall be known and designated as the “William Stafford United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “William Stafford United States Courthouse”.
