# H.R. 2317

> To modernize credit union net worth standards, advance credit union efforts to promote economic growth, and modify and ease credit union regulatory standards and burdens, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Credit Union Regulatory Improvements Act of 2005”.
