# S. 332

> To prohibit the retirement of F–117 Nighthawk stealth attack aircraft during fiscal year 2006.

## § 1. Prohibition on retirement of F–117 Nighthawk stealth attack aircraft

No F–117 Nighthawk stealth attack aircraft in use by the Air Force during fiscal year 2005 may be retired during fiscal year 2006.
