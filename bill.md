# H.R. 3743

> To ensure that certain members of the United States Armed Forces are not subject to secondary airport security screening.

## § 1. Exception for military

Not later than 60 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall issue regulations to ensure that no secondary airport security screening is conducted of a member of the United States Armed Forces who—

- **(1)** is on active duty;
- **(2)** is carrying orders that require the travel such member is engaged in;
- **(3)** is in uniform; and
- **(4)** has valid military identification.
