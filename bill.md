# H.R. 3667

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 200 South Barrington Street in Los Angeles, California, as the “Karl Malden Station”.

## § 1. Designation

The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 200 South Barrington Street in Los Angeles, California, shall be known and designated as the “Karl Malden Station”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Karl Malden Station”.
