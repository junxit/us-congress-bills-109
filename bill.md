# S. 648

> To amend the Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief Act of 1991 to extend the authority for drought assistance.

## § 1. Extension of the reclamation states emergency drought relief act of 1991

Section 104(c) of the Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief Act of 1991 (43 U.S.C. 2214(c)) is amended by striking “September 30, 2005” and inserting “September 30, 2010”.
