# S. 1736

> To provide for the participation of employees in the judicial branch in the Federal leave transfer program for disasters and emergencies.

## § 1. Leave transfer program in disasters and emergencies

Section 6391 of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsection (f) as subsection (g); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (e) the following:
  > - **(f)** After consultation with the Administrative Office of the United States Courts, the Office of Personnel Management shall provide for the participation of employees in the judicial branch in any emergency leave transfer program under this section.
