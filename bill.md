# H. RES. 1087

> Designating Room H–139 of the Capitol as the “Henry J. Hyde Room”.

That—

- **(1)** Room H–139 of the Capitol shall be known and designated as the “Henry J. Hyde Room”; and
- **(2)** this resolution shall take effect on the day following the date on which Henry J. Hyde is no longer serving as a Member of the House of Representatives.
