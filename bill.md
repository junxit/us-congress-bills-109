# H.R. 5793

> To require the Secretary of Defense, in coordination with the Secretary of Homeland Security and State governments, to develop detailed operational plans regarding Defense Support to Civil Authorities missions.

## § 1. Department of Defense operational plans for Armed Forces support for civil authorities

The Secretary of Defense, in coordination with the Secretary of Homeland Security and State governments, shall develop detailed operational plans regarding the use of the Armed Forces to support activities of civil authorities, known as Defense Support to Civil Authorities missions. These plans shall specifically address response options to hurricanes, wildfires, earthquakes, pandemic, and other natural disasters.
