# H.R. 5187

> To amend the John F. Kennedy Center Act to authorize additional appropriations for the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts for fiscal year 2007.

## § 1. John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts

- **(a)** *Maintenance, Repair, and Security* Section 13(a) of the John F. Kennedy Center Act (20 U.S.C. 76r(a)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (1) by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (2) by striking “, 2006, and 2007.” and inserting “and 2006; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(3)** $19,100,000 for fiscal year 2007.

- **(b)** *Capital Projects* Section 13(b) of such Act (20 U.S.C. 76r(b)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (1) by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (2) by striking “, 2006, and 2007.” and inserting “and 2006; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(3)** $20,000,000 for fiscal year 2007.
