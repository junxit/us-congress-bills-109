# H.R. 382

> To amend title 40, United States Code, to designate certain counties and a city as part of the Appalachian region.

## § 1. Additional counties and city in Appalachian region

Section 14102(a)(1) of title 40, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by inserting the following: “and cities located within the boundaries of such area” before the closing parenthesis; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (L)—
  - **(A)** by inserting “Franklin” after “Floyd”;
  - **(B)** by inserting “Henry,” after “Grayson,”;
  - **(C)** by inserting “Patrick,” after “Montgomery,”; and
  - **(D)** by inserting “and the city of Martinsville” after “and Wythe”.
