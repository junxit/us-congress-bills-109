# H.R. 4849

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to provide that individuals who are eligible to join the Armed Forces of the United States are also eligible to be security screening personnel.

That section 44935(e)(2)(A)(ii) of title 49, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(ii)** to be eligible to be a security screener if the individual is eligible to join the Armed Forces of the United States;
