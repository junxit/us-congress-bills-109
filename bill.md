# H.R. 4879

> To reliquidate certain entries of salmon.

## § 1. Certain entries of salmon

- **(a)** *Liquidation or Reliquidation of Entries* Notwithstanding sections 514 and 520 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1514 and 1520) or any other provision of law, the Secretary of the Treasury shall, not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this Act, liquidate or reliquidate the entries listed in subsection (b) without assessment of antidumping duties or interest and shall refund any antidumping duties or interest which were previously paid on such entries.
