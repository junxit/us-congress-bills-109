# H.R. 5048

> To direct the Secretary of Energy to undertake a study of the need for and feasibility of establishing a strategic natural gas reserve.

## § 1. Report

Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Energy shall transmit to the Congress a report on the results of a study of the need for and feasibility of establishing a strategic natural gas reserve similar to the Strategic Petroleum Reserve.
