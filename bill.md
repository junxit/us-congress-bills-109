# H.R. 5398

> To amend the Clean Air Act to exclude from the definition of renewable fuel any fuel that is imported or derived from any matter that is imported.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “USA Fuels Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Exclusion of imported fuel from definition of renewable fuel

Subparagraph (C) of section 211(o)(1) of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7545(o)(1)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(iii)** *Exclusion* The term renewable fuel excludes any fuel described in clause (i) or (ii) that is imported or derived from any matter that is imported.
