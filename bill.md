# H.R. 4467

> To authorize salary adjustments for Justices and judges of the United States for fiscal year 2006.

## § 1. Authorization of salary adjustments for Federal justices and judges

Pursuant to section 140 of Public Law 97–92, Justices and judges of the United States are authorized during fiscal year 2006 to receive a salary adjustment in accordance with section 461 of title 28, United States Code.
