# H.R. 6097

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to allow additional transit systems greater flexibility with certain mass transportation projects.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transit System Flexibility Protection Act of 2006”.

## § 2. General authority

Section 5307(b)(1) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by striking subparagraph (E) and inserting the following:
