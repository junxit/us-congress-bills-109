# S. 2082

> To amend the USA PATRIOT ACT to extend the sunset of certain provisions of that Act and the lone wolf provision of the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 to March 31, 2006.

## § 1. Extension of sunset of certain provisions of the USA PATRIOT ACT and the lone wolf provision of the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004

Section 224(a) of the Uniting and Strengthening America by Providing Appropriate Tools Required to Intercept and Obstruct Terrorism (USA PATRIOT ACT) Act of 2001 (Public Law 107–56; 115 Stat. 295) is amended by striking “December 31, 2005” and inserting “March 31, 2006”.
