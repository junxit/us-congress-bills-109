# H.R. 3153

> To reauthorize the Upper Colorado and San Juan River Basin endangered fish recovery implementation programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Upper Colorado and San Juan Basin Endangered Fish Recovery Implementation Programs Reauthorization Act of 2005”.
