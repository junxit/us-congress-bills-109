# H.R. 3473

> To amend the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 to apply to Federal and State government employers.

## § 1. Federal and State government employers

Section 3(5) of the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 is amended by striking “but does not include” and all that follows through “State” and inserting “including the United States government, any government of a State or political subdivision of a State, or any interstate governmental agency.”
