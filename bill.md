# H.R. 3846

> To amend the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 to extend the Milk Income Loss Contract Program through the end of calendar year 2005.

## § 1. Extension of contracts under Milk Income Loss Contract Program

Section 1502 of the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 (7 U.S.C. 7982) is amended in subsections (f) and (g)(1) by striking “September 30, 2005” and inserting “December 31, 2005”.
