# S. 1977

> To repeal section 5 of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972.

## § 1. Repeal of section 5 of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972

Section 5 of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 (33 U.S.C. 476) is repealed.
