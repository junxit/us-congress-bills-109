# H. RES. 284

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to repeal the provisional quorum provision.

That paragraph (c) of clause 5 of rule XX of the Rules of the House of Representatives (relating to provisional quorums) is repealed and that paragraph (d) of such clause is redesignated as paragraph (c).
