# S. 1949

> To provide for coordination of proliferation interdiction activities and conventional arms disarmament, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cooperative Proliferation Detection, Interdiction Assistance, and Conventional Threat Reduction Act of 2005”.
