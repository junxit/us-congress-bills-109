# S. 643

> To amend the Agricultural Credit Act of 1987 to reauthorize State mediation programs.

## § 1. Reauthorization of State mediation programs

Section 506 of the Agricultural Credit Act of 1987 (7 U.S.C. 5106) is amended by striking “2005” and inserting “2010”.
