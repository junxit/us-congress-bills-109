# S. 871

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to ensure that the strength of the Armed Forces and the protections and benefits for members of the Armed Forces and their families are adequate for keeping the commitment of the people of the United States to support their servicemembers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Standing With Our Troops Act of 2005”.
