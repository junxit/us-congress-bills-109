# H.R. 3497

> To ensure that when Federal law allows a court to award attorney fees to one party, the law must allow the court to award attorney fees to any party.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Loser Pays Legal Fee Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Reciprocity in awards of attorney fees

- **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, whenever Federal law provides a basis on which a court may award attorney fees to one prevailing party, the court may award attorney fees to any prevailing party on that basis.
- **(b)** *Exception* Subsection (a) does not apply to a Federal law that provides a basis on which a court may award attorney fees only to the United States.
