# H.R. 4701

> To amend the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) to extend the authorization of appropriations for the Office of Government Ethics through fiscal year 2011.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Office of Government Ethics Authorization Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Extension of authorization of appropriations

Section 405 of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) is amended by striking “2002 through 2006” and inserting “2007 through 2011”.
