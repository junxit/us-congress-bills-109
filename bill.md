# S. 2038

> To amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 to restore the original deadline for mandatory country of origin labeling.

## § 1. Country of origin labeling

Section 285 of the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 (7 U.S.C. 1638d) is amended by striking “2008” and inserting “2006”.
