# S. 1505

> To amend the Shawnee Tribe Status Act of 2000.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Shawnee Tribe Status Act Amendments Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Tribal land

Section 707(a) of the Shawnee Tribe Status Act of 2000 (25 U.S.C. 1041e(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1), by striking “(1) In general.—”; and
- **(2)** by striking paragraph (2).
