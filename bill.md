# S. 2320

> To make available funds included in the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005 for the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program for fiscal year 2006, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Funds for Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program

Section 9001 of the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005 is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by striking “for a 1-time only obligation and expenditure”;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (1), by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “fiscal year 2006”; and
  - **(C)** in paragraph (2), by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “fiscal year 2006”;
- **(2)** by redesignating subsection (b) as subsection (c);
- **(3)** by inserting after subsection (a) the following:
  > - **(b)** *Limitation* None of the funds made available under this section may be used for the planning and administering described in section 2605(b)(9) of the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981 (42 U.S.C. 8624(b)(9)).

- **(4)** in subsection (c) (as redesignated by paragraph (2)), by striking “September 30, 2007” and inserting “September 30, 2006”.
