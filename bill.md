# H.R. 2132

> To extend the waiver authority of the Secretary of Education with respect to student financial assistance during a war or other military operation or national emergency.

## § 1. Extension of waiver authority

Section 6 of the Higher Education Relief Opportunities for Students Act of 2003 (20 U.S.C. 1070, note) is amended by striking “September 30, 2005” and inserting “September 30, 2007”.
