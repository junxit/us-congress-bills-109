# H.R. 2929

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to provide the same type of vocational rehabilitation benefits and services under laws administered by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs for certain children with spina bifida as are currently available to veterans with service-connected disabilities.

## § 1. Vocational rehabilitation for children with spina bifida

Section 1804(c)(1) of title 38, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(c)** (1) Subject to paragraph (2), a vocational training program for a child under this section may include the services and assistance described in section 3104 of this title.
