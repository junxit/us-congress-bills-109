# S. 2549

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to expand the use of health savings accounts for the payment of health insurance premiums for high deductible health plans purchased in the individual market.

## § 1. Short title

-  This Act may be cited as the “Health Savings Account Affordability Act”.

## § 2. Expansion of use of health savings accounts for health insurance premium payments for certain high deductible health plans

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (C) of section 223(d)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to qualified medical expenses) is amended by striking “or” at the end of clause (iii), by striking the period at the end of clause (iv), and by adding at the end the following new clause:
  > - **(v)** any high deductible health plan other than a group health plan (as defined in section 5000(b)(1)).

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2006.
