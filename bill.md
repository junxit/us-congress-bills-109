# H.R. 4912

> To amend section 242 of the National Housing Act to extend the exemption for critical access hospitals under the FHA program for mortgage insurance for hospitals.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Rural Health Care Capital Access Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Extension

Paragraph (1) of section 242(i) of the National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1715z–7(i)(1)) is amended by striking “July 31, 2006” and inserting “July 31, 2011”.
