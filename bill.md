# H.R. 5619

> To extend the patent term for the badge of the American Legion, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Patent Term Extension for the Badge of the American Legion

The term of a certain design patent numbered 54,296 (for the badge of the American Legion) is renewed and extended for a period of 14 years beginning on the date of enactment of this Act, with all the rights and privileges pertaining to such patent.
