# S. 3886

> To authorize military commissions to bring terrorists to justice, to strengthen and modernize terrorist surveillance capabilities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Terrorist Tracking, Identification, and Prosecution Act of 2006”.
