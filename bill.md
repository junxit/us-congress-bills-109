# H.R. 5826

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to provide for greater flexibility in the number of levels of benefits that may be offered by certain health plans under chapter 89 of such title.

## § 1. Amendment

Section 8903 of title 5, United States Code, is amended in paragraphs (1) and (2) by inserting “at least” before “two levels of benefits”.
