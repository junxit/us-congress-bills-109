# S. 4050

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 103 East Thompson Street in Thomaston, Georgia, as the “Sergeant First Class Robert Lee “Bobby” Hollar, Jr. Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Sergeant first class Robert Lee “Bobby” Hollar, Jr. post office building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 103 East Thompson Street in Thomaston, Georgia, shall be known and designated as the “Sergeant First Class Robert Lee “Bobby” Hollar, Jr. Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Sergeant First Class Robert Lee “Bobby” Hollar, Jr. Post Office Building”.
