# H.R. 5415

> For the relief of Vernadette Bader.

## § 1. Payment by the Secretary of the Treasury

The Secretary of the Treasury shall pay to Vernadette Bader, of Taylor, Michigan, out of any amounts in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $11,202.68, as compensation for an erroneous calculation made by the National Labor Relations Board in the distribution of funds from an employment discrimination settlement between Taylor Machine Products, Incorporated, and 7 former employees of that corporation, including Vernadette Bader.

## § 2. Satisfaction of claims

The payment made pursuant to section 1 shall be in full satisfaction of all claims Vernadette Bader may have against the United States for the economic injuries received by her as a result of the erroneous calculation described in section 1.

## § 3. Limitation on attorney and agent fees

Not more than an amount equal to 10 percent of the amount paid pursuant to section 1 may be paid to or received by any attorney or agent for services rendered to Vernadette Bader with respect to the compensation referred to in section 1. Any person who violates this section shall be subject to a fine under title 18, United States Code.
