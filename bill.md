# H.R. 2003

> To amend the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to remove the 100 percent tariff imposed on Roquefort cheese.

## § 1. Removal of 100 percent tariff on Roquefort cheese

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter III of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by striking heading 9903.02.30.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendments* Chapter 4 of such Schedule is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking footnote 1 to subheading 0406.40.20; and
  - **(2)** by striking footnote 1 to subheading 0406.40.40.
