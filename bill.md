## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Additional coverage* The term additional coverage has the meaning given the term in section 502(b)(1) of the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 U.S.C. 1502(b)(1)).
- **(2)** *Insurable commodity* The term insurable commodity means an agricultural commodity (excluding livestock) for which the producers on a farm are eligible to obtain a policy or plan of insurance under the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 U.S.C. 1501 et seq.).
- **(3)** *Livestock* The term livestock includes—
  - **(A)** cattle;
  - **(B)** bison;
  - **(C)** sheep;
  - **(D)** swine; and
  - **(E)** other livestock, as determined by the Secretary.
- **(4)** *Noninsurable commodity* The term noninsurable commodity means a crop for which the producers on a farm are eligible to obtain assistance under section 196 of the Federal Agriculture Improvement and Reform Act of 1996 (7 U.S.C. 7333).
- **(5)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of Agriculture.
