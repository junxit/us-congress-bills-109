# H.R. 5535

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to exclude foreign governmental entities from suing under the treble damages portion of its civil RICO provisions.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Prevention of Civil RICO Abuse Act of 2006”.

## § 2. RICO amendment

Section 1964(c) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following: “A foreign governmental entity may not sue under this subsection.”.
