# H.R. 2900

> To enable the residents of the Bayshore Manor assisted living facility in Key West, Florida, to continue to receive supplemental security income benefits under title XVI of the Social Security Act.

## § 1. Exemption for the Bayshore Manor assisted living facility in Key West, Florida

For purposes of the supplemental security income program under title XVI of the Social Security Act, the Bayshore Manor assisted living facility in Key West, Florida, shall not be considered to be a public institution within the meaning of section 1611(e)(1) of such Act.
