# H. RES. 206

> Recognizing the 100th anniversary of Garner, North Carolina.

## Preamble

- Whereas, in 2005, Garner, North Carolina, will celebrate the 100th anniversary of its founding;
- Whereas history shows that there were settlers in the area before the Revolutionary War, including documentation of a church in the Panther Branch Township in 1756;
- Whereas, in 1905, community leaders applied to the North Carolina General Assembly for a town charter, which was granted on April 16th, 1905;
- Whereas the first business in Garner is said to have been a wood shop owned by Henry Fort, an African-American cabinet maker whose work is still in use in many local homes;
- Whereas Garner has grown to become a dynamic, thriving community; and
- Whereas, on April 16, 2005, Garner will celebrate its achievements and culture with a Centennial Day Celebration: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives recognizes the 100th anniversary of Garner, North Carolina.
