# S. 2386

> To suspend temporarily the duty on 1-Flouro-2-nitrobenzene.

## § 1. 1-Flouro-2-nitrobenzene

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by striking heading 9902.29.04 and inserting the following new heading:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
