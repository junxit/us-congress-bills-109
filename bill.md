# H.R. 4586

> To extend the life of the Benjamin Franklin Tercentenary Commission.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the “Benjamin Franklin Tercentenary Commission Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Benjamin Franklin Tercentenary Commission

Section 9(b) of the Benjamin Franklin Tercentenary Commission Act (Public Law 107–202; 36 U.S.C. note prec. 101) is amended by striking “not later than January 16, 2007” and inserting “not later than January 16, 2009”.
