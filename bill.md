# H.R. 3765

> To extend through December 31, 2007, the authority of the Secretary of the Army to accept and expend funds contributed by non-Federal public entities to expedite the processing of permits.

## § 1. Funding to process permits

Section 214 of the Water Resources Development Act of 2000 (33 U.S.C. 2201 note; 114 Stat. 2594; 117 Stat. 1836) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a) by striking “In fiscal years 2001 through 2005, the” and inserting “The”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(c)** *Duration of Authority* The authority provided under this section shall be in effect from October 1, 2000, through December 31, 2007.
