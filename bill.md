# H.R. 3974

> To prohibit the closure or relocation of county or local Farm Service Agency offices pending the completion of the next omnibus agriculture law.

## § 1. Prohibition on closure or relocation of Farm Service Agency offices

The Secretary of Agriculture may not close or relocate a county or local Farm Service Agency office before the later of—

- **(1)** October 1, 2007; or
- **(2)** the date of the enactment of an omnibus Act authorizing funds for programs of the Department of Agriculture for fiscal year 2008.
