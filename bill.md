# H. RES. 628

> Congratulating Bruce Springsteen of New Jersey on the 30th anniversary of his masterpiece record album “Born to Run,” and commending him on a career that has touched the lives of millions of Americans.

## Preamble

- Whereas Bruce Springsteen is a native of New Jersey whose music has given voice to the hopes and dreams of the American people;
- Whereas Mr. Springsteen honed his talent playing in venues along the Jersey Shore;
- Whereas Mr. Springsteen achieved widespread and well-deserved recognition 30 years ago with the release of his 1975 classic rock album, “Born to Run”;
- Whereas Mr. Springsteen tells his stories in the language of his native State and the Nation; and
- Whereas while the appeal of Bruce Springsteen extends far beyond the Garden State, he still holds a special place in the hearts of New Jerseyans: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives congratulates Bruce Springsteen of New Jersey on the 30th anniversary of his masterpiece record album, “Born to Run”, and commends him on a career that has touched the lives of millions of Americans.
