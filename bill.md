# S. 344

> To link recidivist penalties for certain drug crimes.

## § 1. Prior offenses

- **(a)** *Distribution to minors* Section 418(b) of the Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 859(b)) is amended by inserting “, section 419, or section 420” after “subsection (a) of this section”.
- **(b)** *Distribution near schools* Section 419(b) of the Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 860(b)) is amended by inserting “, section 418, or section 420” after “subsection (a) of this section”.
- **(c)** *Employment of minors* Section 420(c) of the Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 861(c)) is amended by inserting “, section 418, or section 419” after “subsection (a) of this section”.
