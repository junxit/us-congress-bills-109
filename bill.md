# H.R. 183

> To make the repeal of the estate tax permanent.

## § 1. Estate tax repeal made permanent

- **(a)** *In general* Section 901 of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a) by striking “shall not apply—” and all that follows and inserting “(other than title V) shall not apply to taxable, plan, or limitation years beginning after December 31, 2010.”, and
  - **(2)** in subsection (b) by striking “, estates, gifts, and transfers”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall take effect as if included in section 901 of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001.
