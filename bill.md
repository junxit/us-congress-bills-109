# H.R. 1032

> To eliminate the termination date on authority for schools with low default rates to make single disbursements of student loans.

## § 1. Elimination of termination date

Section 422(d) of the Higher Education Amendments of 1998 (112 Stat. 1696) is amended by striking “, and ending September 30, 2002”.
