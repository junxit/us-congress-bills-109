# H.R. 4134

> To provide that rates of pay for Members of Congress shall not be increased as a result of any adjustment otherwise scheduled to take effect in fiscal year 2006.

## § 1. No cost of living adjustment for Members of Congress

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no adjustment shall be made under section 601(a) of the Legislative Reorganization Act of 1946 (2 U.S.C. 31), relating to cost of living adjustments for Members of Congress, during fiscal year 2006.
