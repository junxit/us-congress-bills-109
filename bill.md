# H.R. 4321

> To repeal the amendment made by section 796 of the Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2006, exempting from harboring sanctions compensation for alien volunteers for certain religious organizations.

## § 1. Repeal of immigration harboring exception for compensation of alien volunteers by certain bona fide, nonprofit religious organizations

Effective as if included in the enactment of the Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2006, section 274(a)(1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1324(a)(1)) is amended by striking subparagraph (C), as added by section 796 of such Appropriations Act.
