# H.R. 3684

> To suspend the Davis-Bacon wage rate requirements for Federal contracts in areas declared national disasters.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the “Cleanup and Reconstruction Enhancement Act (CARE Act)”.

## § 2. Suspension of Davis-Bacon wage requirements in national disaster areas

Section 3147 of title 40, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following: “In any area that the President determines to be a major disaster under section 102(2) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5122(2)), the provisions of this subchapter shall not apply for a period of 1 year from the date on which the President makes such determination.”
