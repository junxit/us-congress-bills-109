# H.R. 5029

> To establish in the Department of Homeland Security a Domestic Nuclear Detection Office to improve the ability of the United States to detect and prevent acts of nuclear and radiological terrorism and to enhance coordination of such efforts across Federal agencies, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Prevention of Nuclear Terrorism Act of 2006”.
