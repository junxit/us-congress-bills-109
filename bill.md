# H.R. 1678

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the deduction for increased expensing for small business.

## § 1. Extension of deduction for increased expensing for small business

Subsections (b), (c), and (d) of section 179 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to election to expense certain depreciable business assets) are each amended by striking ‘‘2008’’ each place it appears and inserting ‘‘2010’’.
