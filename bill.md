# H.R. 4664

> To amend the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to reduce certain contribution limits under such Act.

## § 1. Reduction of Limit on Maximum Amount of Certain Contributions

- **(a)** *Contributions to Candidates* Section 315(a)(1)(A) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 441a(a)(1)(A)) is amended by striking “$2,000” and inserting “$1,000”.
- **(b)** *Contributions to Nonparty Political Committees* Section 315(a)(1)(C) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 441a(a)(1)(C)) is amended by striking “$5,000” and inserting “$1,000”.
- **(c)** *Contributions by Multicandidate Political Committees to Candidates* Section 315(a)(2)(A) of such Act (2 U.S.C. 441a(a)(2)) is amended by striking “$5,000” and inserting “$1,000”.

## § 2. Delay in Implementation of Indexing of Limit on Maximum Amount of Contribution to Candidate; Application of Indexing to Certain Other Limits

- **(a)** *Application to Limits on Contributions to Nonparty Political Committees and Contributions by Multicandidate Political Committees to Candidates* Section 315(c)(1) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 441a(c)(1)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (B)(i), by inserting “(a)(1)(C), (a)(2)(A),” after “(a)(1)(B),”; and
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (C), by inserting “(a)(1)(C), (a)(2)(A),” after “(a)(1)(B),”.
- **(b)** *Timing of Indexing*
  - **(1)** *Delay in indexing for certain limits* Section 315(c)(1)(B) of such Act (2 U.S.C. 441a(c)(1)(B)) is amended by striking “2002” and inserting “2002 (or, in the case of the limitation established by subsections (a)(1)(A), (a)(1)(C), or (a)(2)(A), 2008)”.
  - **(2)** *Determination of base period* Section 315(c)(2)(B) of such Act (2 U.S.C. 441a(c)(2)(B)) is amended—
    - **(A)** in clause (i), by striking “and” at the end;
    - **(B)** in clause (ii)—
      - **(i)** by striking “(a)(1)(A),”, and
      - **(ii)** by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
    - **(C)** by adding at the end the following new clause:
      > - **(iii)** for purposes of subsections (a)(1)(A), (a)(1)(C), and (a)(2)(A), calendar year 2007.

## § 3. Effective Date

The amendments made by this Act shall apply with respect to elections for Federal office occurring after December 2006.
