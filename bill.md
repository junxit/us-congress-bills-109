# H.R. 1271

> To repeal a provision relating to privacy officers in the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2005.

## § 1. Repeal of Privacy Officer provision

Section 522 of the Transportation, Treasury, Independent Agencies, and General Government Appropriations Act, 2005 (Public Law 108–447; 118 Stat. 3268; 5 U.S.C. 552a note), is hereby repealed.
