# H.R. 169

> To amend the Intermodal Surface Transportation Efficiency Act of 1991 to designate a high priority corridor in California.

## § 1. California high priority corridor

Section 1105(c) of the Intermodal Surface Transportation Efficiency Act of 1991 (105 Stat. 2031; 109 Stat. 596; 112 Stat. 190) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(46)** I–710 between the terminus at Long Beach, California, to State Route 60 in California.
