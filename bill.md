# S. 2176

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to modify eligibility for income replacement payments for Reserves experiencing extended and frequent mobilization for active duty service.

## § 1. Modification of eligibility for income replacement payments for Reserves experiencing extended and frequent mobilization for active duty service

Subsection (b)(1) of section 910 of title 10, United States Code, as added by section 614 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2006 (Public Law 109–__), is amended by striking “18 continuous months of service” and inserting “six continuous months of service”.
