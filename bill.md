# H.R. 3770

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 205 West Washington Street in Knox, Indiana, as the “Grant W. Green Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Grant W. Green Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 205 West Washington Street in Knox, Indiana, shall be known and designated as the “Grant W. Green Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Grant W. Green Post Office Building”.
