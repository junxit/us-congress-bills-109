# H. RES. 955

> Calling for sustainable peace in the Middle East.

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** calls on all parties in the Middle East to observe a cease-fire and renew efforts to create a sustainable peace;
- **(2)** calls on the United States and the international community to assist in the reconstruction and rebuilding efforts in areas in the Middle East that are affected by violence; and
- **(3)** urges all parties to recognize the right of the State of Israel to exist.
