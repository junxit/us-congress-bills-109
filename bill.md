# S. 1166

> To extend the authorization of the Kalaupapa National Historical Park Advisory Commission.

## § 1. Extension of authorization

Section 108(e) of the Act entitled “An Act to establish the Kalaupapa National Historical Park in the State of Hawaii, and for other purposes” (16 U.S.C. 410jj–7) is amended by striking “twenty-five years from” and inserting “on the date that is 45 years after”.
