# H.R. 3425

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to require mandatory HIV testing of potential sources in the event of possible occupational exposure to HIV in a Department of Veterans Affairs medical facility.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veterans Health Administration Employee Protection Act”.
