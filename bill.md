# H.R. 1941

> To reduce temporarily the duty on triethylene glycol bis[3-(3-tert-butyl-4-hydroxy-5-methylphenyl)propionate].

## § 1. Triethylene glycol bis[3-(3-tert-butyl-4-hydroxy-5-methylphenyl)propionate]

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.01.88 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “Free” and inserting “4.1%”; and
  - **(2)** in the effective period column, by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2008”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
