# H.R. 4805

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 105 North Quincy Street in Clinton, Illinois, as the “Gene Vance Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Designation

The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 105 North Quincy Street in Clinton, Illinois, shall be known and designated as the “Gene Vance Post Office Building”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Gene Vance Post Office Building”.
