# S. 2273

> To make available funds included in the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005 for the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981 program for fiscal year 2006, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Amendment to the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005

Section 9001 of the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005 is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by striking “for a 1-time only obligation and expenditure”;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (1), by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “fiscal year 2006”; and
  - **(C)** in paragraph (2), by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “fiscal year 2006”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (b), by striking “September 30, 2007” and inserting “September 30, 2006”.
