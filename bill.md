# S. 3042

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to improve preparedness for and response to bioterrorism and other public health emergencies, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “All-Hazards Public Health Emergency and Bioterrorism Preparedness and Response Act”.
