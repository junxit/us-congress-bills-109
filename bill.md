# H.R. 3457

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to provide for World War II veterans to be in the same priority category for health care services from the Department of Veterans Affairs as World War I veterans.

## § 1. Priority category for Department of Veterans Affairs health care services for World War II veterans

Section 1710(a)(2)(E) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting “or World War II” after “World War I”.
