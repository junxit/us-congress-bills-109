# S. 1409

> To amend the Safe Drinking Water Act Amendments of 1996 to modify the grant program to improve sanitation in rural and Native villages in the State of Alaska.

## § 1. Grants to Alaska to improve sanitation in rural and Native villages

Section 303 of the Safe Drinking Water Act Amendments of 1996 (33 U.S.C. 1263a) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b), by striking “50 percent” and inserting “75 percent”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (e)—
  - **(A)** by striking “$40,000,000” and inserting “$45,000,000”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “2005” and inserting “2010”.
