# H.R. 6302

> To remove the frequency limitation on Medicare coverage for intermittent catheterization.

## § 1. Removing frequency limitation on Medicare coverage for intermittent catheterization

Effective January 1, 2007, the Secretary of Health and Human Services in making coverage determinations respecting intermittent catheterization under part B of title XVIII of the Social Security Act shall not deny coverage of a replacement catheter on the basis of frequency of use.
