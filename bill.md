# H.R. 2897

> To amend title 4, United States Code, to provide for the proper display of the flag over a one-way street.

## § 1. Display of flag over one-way street

Section 7(j) of title 4, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “of the street” and inserting “of a two-way street”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following: “When the flag is displayed over the middle of a one-way street, it should be suspended vertically with the union to the left of an observer moving in the direction of traffic.”.
