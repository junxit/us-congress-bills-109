# H.J. RES. 69

> Relating to a national emergency declared by the President on September 8, 2005.

That, pursuant to section 202 of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. 1622), the national emergency declared by the finding of the President on September 8, 2005, in Proclamation 7924 (70 Fed. Reg. 54227) is hereby terminated.
