# S. 2241

> For the relief of Carmen Shahrzad Kulcsar.

## § 1. Permanent residence

Notwithstanding any other provision of law or any order, for purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101 et seq.), Carmen Shahrzad Kulcsar shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of enactment of this Act upon the payment of the required visa fees.

## § 2. Reduction of number of available visas

Upon the granting of permanent residence to Carmen Shahrzad Kulcsar under section 1, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper officer to reduce by 1 the total number of immigrant visas available during the current fiscal year to natives of the country of the alien’s birth under section 203(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1153(a)).
