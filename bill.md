# S. 621

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to permanently extend the 15-year recovery period for the depreciation of certain leasehold improvements.

## § 1. Permanent extension of 15-year recovery period for depreciation of certain leasehold improvements

Section 168(e)(3)(E)(iv) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (defining 15-year property) is amended by striking “before January 1, 2006”.
