# H. RES. 1043

> Directing the Director of National Intelligence to submit to the House of Representatives not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, in unclassified form, all documents in the possession of the Director concerning the impact of the war in Iraq on terrorism and terrorist threats, including a National Intelligence Estimates entitled “Trends in Global Terrorism: Implications for the United States” dated on or about April, 2006, and any other actual or pending National Intelligence Estimates concerning Iraq.

That the Director of National Intelligence is directed to submit to the House of Representatives not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, in unclassified form, all documents in the possession of the Director concerning the impact of the war in Iraq on terrorism and terrorist threats, including a National Intelligence Estimates entitled “Trends in Global Terrorism: Implications for the United States,” dated on or about April, 2006, and any other actual or pending National Intelligence Estimates concerning Iraq.
