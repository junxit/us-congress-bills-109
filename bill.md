# S. 438

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to repeal the medicare outpatient rehabilitation therapy caps.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medicare Access to Rehabilitation Services Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Outpatient therapy cap repeal

Section 1833 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395(l)) is amended by striking subsection (g).
