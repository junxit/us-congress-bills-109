# S. 1793

> To extend certain apportionments to primary airports.

## § 1. Extension of apportionments

Section 47114(c)(1)(F) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by striking “and 2005” each place it appears in the text and in the heading and inserting “, 2005, and 2006”.
