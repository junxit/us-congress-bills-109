# H.R. 667

> To direct the Secretary of the Army to carry out the dredging project, Menominee Harbor, Menominee River, Michigan and Wisconsin.

That the project for navigation, Menominee Harbor and River, Michigan and Wisconsin, deauthorized on April 15, 2002, in accordance with section 1001(b)(2) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1986 (33 U.S.C. 579a(b)(2)), shall be carried out by the Secretary of the Army as provided for in section 101 of the River and Harbor Act of 1960 (74 Stat. 482) at Federal expense, notwithstanding that the benefits of the project may not exceed the estimated costs of the project.
