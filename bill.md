# S. 903

> To provide for the correction of a certain John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources System map.

## § 1. Corrections to John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources System map

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Interior shall make any corrections to the map described in subsection (b) that are necessary to ensure that the map is consistent with the map entitled “Corrections to Coastal Barrier Resources System Map Unit T–10”, dated June _____, 2004, and on file with the Secretary and the Committee on Environment and Public Works of the Senate.
- **(b)** *Map described* The map described in this subsection is the map that is subtitled “Four Mile Hill Unit TX–16P, North Padre Island Unit T10/T10P”, which is included in the set of maps entitled “Coastal Barrier Resources System” and dated October 24, 1990.
- **(c)** *Availability* The Secretary of the Interior shall keep the map described in subsection (b) on file and available for public inspection in accordance with section 4(b) of the Coastal Barrier Resources Act (16 U.S.C. 3503(b)).
