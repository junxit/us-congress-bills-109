# S. 1416

> To amend the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act to reauthorize the Interagency Council on Homelessness.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Interagency Council on Homelessness Reauthorization Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

- **(a)** *Authorization of appropriations* Section 208 of the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 11318) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “1993” and inserting “2006”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “1994” and inserting “2007”.
- **(b)** *Termination* Section 209 of such Act (42 U.S.C. 11319) is amended by striking “2005” and inserting “2007”.
