# H.R. 1967

> To extend the suspension of duty on Acid black 172.

## § 1. Acid black 172

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.01.75 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Acid black 172) is amended by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2008”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
