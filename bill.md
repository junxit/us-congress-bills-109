# H. RES. 1023

> Requesting the President to provide certain information to the House of Representatives relating to Maher Arar.

That the President is requested to provide to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, all documents and records in the possession of the President relating to Maher Arar.
