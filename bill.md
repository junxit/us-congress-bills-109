# H.R. 5539

> To reauthorize the North American Wetlands Conservation Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “North American Wetlands Conservation Reauthorization Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

Section 7(c) of the North American Wetlands Conservation Act (16 U.S.C. 4406(c)) is amended by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “each of fiscal years 2008 through 2012”.
