# H.R. 692

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to provide that the Civil Service Retirement and Disability Fund be excluded from the Federal budget.

## § 1. Exclusion of Civil Service Retirement and Disability Fund from the budget

Section 8348 of title 5, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(m)** Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the receipts and disbursements of the Fund—
>   - **(1)** shall not be included in the totals of—
>     - **(A)** the budget of the United States Government as submitted by the President; or
>     - **(B)** the congressional budget (including allocations of budget authority and outlays provided therein); and
>   - **(2)** shall be exempt from any general budget limitation imposed by statute on expenditures and net lending (budget outlays) of the United States Government.
