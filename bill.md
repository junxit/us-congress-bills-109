# H. RES. 549

> Requesting the President of the United States provide to the House of Representatives all documents in his possession relating to his October 7, 2002, speech in Cincinnati, Ohio, and his January 28, 2003, State of the Union address.

That the President of the United States is requested to provide to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of adoption of this resolution—

- **(1)** all drafts of his October 7, 2002, speech in Cincinnati, Ohio, and all documents in his possession related to that speech; and
- **(2)** all drafts of his January 28, 2003, State of the Union address and all documents in his possession related to that address.
