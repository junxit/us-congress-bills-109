# H.R. 3172

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to repeal the security screening opt-out program for airport operators.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Passenger Security Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Security screening opt-out program

- **(a)** *Repeal* Section 44920 of title 49, United States Code, and the item relating to such section in the analysis for chapter 449 of such title, are repealed.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendments* Chapter 449 of such title is amended—
  - **(1)** in section 44901(a) by striking “or 44920”; and
  - **(2)** by striking section 44919(i).
