# H.R. 283

> To amend the Safe and Drug-Free Schools and Communities Act and the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to authorize the use of grant funds for bullying and gang prevention, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Bullying and Gang Prevention for School Safety and Crime Reduction Act of 2005”.
