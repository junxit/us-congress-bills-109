# S. 3404

> To reauthorize the Mni Wiconi Rural Water Supply Project.

## § 1. Mni Wiconi Rural Water Supply Project, South Dakota

Section 10(a) of the Mni Wiconi Project Act of 1988 (Public Law 100–516; 102 Stat. 2571; 116 Stat. 3033) is amended in the second sentence by striking “2008” and inserting “2012”.
