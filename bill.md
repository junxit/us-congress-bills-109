# H.R. 5431

> To amend the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to extend the tariff duties on ethanol.

## § 1. Extension of tariff duties on ethanol

Headings 9901.00.50 and 9901.00.52 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (19 U.S.C. 3007) are each amended in the effective period column by striking “10/1/2007” each place it appears and inserting “1/1/2011”.
