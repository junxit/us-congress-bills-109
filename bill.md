# S. 483

> To strengthen religious liberty and combat government hostility to expressions of faith, by extending the reach of The Equal Access Act to elementary schools.

## § 1. Equal access for elementary schools

The Equal Access Act (20 U.S.C. 4071 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 802—
  - **(A)** in subsection (a), by inserting “elementary school or” after “public”; and
  - **(B)** in subsection (b), by inserting “elementary school or” after “public”; and
- **(2)** in section 803, by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(5)** The term elementary school means a public school that provides elementary education as determined by State law.
