# S. 35

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the credit for production of electricity from wind.

## § 1. Extension of credit for production of electricity from wind

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 45(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to wind facility) is amended by striking “January 1, 2006” and inserting “January 1, 2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to electricity produced and sold after the date of the enactment of this Act, in taxable years ending after such date.
