# S. 2284

> To extend the termination date for the exemption of returning workers from the numerical limitations for temporary workers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Save Our Small and Seasonal Businesses Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Extension of returning worker exemption

Section 402(b)(1) of the Save Our Small and Seasonal Businesses Act of 2005 (title IV of division B of Public Law 109–13; 8 U.S.C. 1184 note) is amended by striking “2006” and inserting “2009”.
