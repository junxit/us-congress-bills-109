# H.R. 4136

> To ensure that exports of Alaskan North Slope crude oil are prohibited.

## § 1. Reimposition of prohibition on exports of Alaskan North Slope crude oil

- **(a)** *Repeal of provision authorizing exports* Subsection (s) of section 28 of the Mineral Leasing Act (30 U.S.C. 185(s)) is repealed.
- **(b)** *Reimposition of prohibition on exports* Subsection (d) of section 7 of the Export Administration Act of 1979 (50 U.S.C. App. 2406(d)), and those provisions of that Act relating to the enforcement of such subsection, shall be effective, notwithstanding section 20 of that Act or any other provision of law that would otherwise allow exports of oil to which such section 7(d) applies.
