# H.R. 3992

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on Solvent Blue 104.

## § 1. Solvent Blue 104

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.32.72 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Solvent Blue 104) is amended by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2008”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
