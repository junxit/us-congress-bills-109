# H.R. 416

> To prohibit the use of Department of Defense funds for any study related to the transportation of chemical munitions across State lines.

## § 1. Prohibition on use of Department of Defense funds for study of interstate transportation of chemical munitions

Funds available to the Department of Defense may not be obligated or expended for any study related to the transportation of chemical munitions across State boundaries.
