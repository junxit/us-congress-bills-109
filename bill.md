# H.R. 12

> To amend the Education Land Grant Act to require the Secretary of Agriculture to pay the costs of environmental reviews with respect to conveyances under that Act.

## § 1. Costs of reviews for conveyances under Education Land Grant Act

Section 202 of the Education Land Grant Act (16 U.S.C. 479a) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(f)** *Costs of review* The Secretary shall pay the costs of all actions required under section 102(2)(C) of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4332(2)(C)) with respect to any conveyance under this section.
