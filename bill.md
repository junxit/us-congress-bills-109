# H.R. 699

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to include podiatrists as physicians for purposes of covering physicians services under the Medicaid Program.

## § 1. Including podiatrists as physicians under the Medicaid Program

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1905(a)(5)(A) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396d(a)(5)(A)) is amended by striking “section 1861(r)(1)” and inserting “paragraphs (1) and (3) of section 1861(r)”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to services furnished on or after January 1, 2006.
