# S. 443

> To improve the investigation of criminal antitrust offenses.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Antitrust Criminal Investigative Improvements Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Amendment of predicate crimes for authorization for interception of wire, oral, and electronic communications

Section 2516(1) of title 18, United State Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (q), by striking “or” after the semicolon;
- **(2)** by redesignating subparagraph (r) as subparagraph (s); and
- **(3)** by adding after subparagraph (q) the following:
  > - **(r)** any criminal violation of section 1 (relating to illegal restraints of trade or commerce), 2 (relating to illegal monopolizing of trade or commerce), or 3 (relating to illegal restraints of trade or commerce in territories or the District of Columbia) of the Sherman Act (15 U.S.C. 1, 2, 3); or
