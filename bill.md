# H.R. 3718

> To amend the Energy Policy Act of 2005 to require the Federal Trade Commission to submit to Congress a report on gasoline prices by November 8, 2005.

## § 1. Amendment

Section 1809(c)(1) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 is amended by striking “On completion of the investigation under subsection (a)” and inserting “Not later than November 8, 2005”.
