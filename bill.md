# S. 1514

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the medicine and drugs limitation on the deduction for medical care.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “OTC Medicine Tax Fairness Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Repeal of medicine and drugs limitation on deduction for medical care

- **(a)** *In general* Section 213 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to medical, dental, etc., expenses) is amended by striking subsection (b).
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 213(d) of such Code is amended by striking paragraph (3).
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
