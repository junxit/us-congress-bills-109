# S. 303

> To amend the Social Security Act and the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exempt certain employment as a member of a local governing board from social security coverage.

## § 1. Exemption of certain local governing board employment from social security coverage

- **(a)** *Amendment to Social Security Act* Section 210(a)(7)(F) of the Social Security Act is amended by striking “or” at the end of clause (iv), by adding “or” at the end of clause (v), and by inserting after clause (v) the following new clause:
  > - **(vi)** by any elected or appointed member of the general governing board of any political subdivision of a State if the remuneration paid in a calendar year for such service is not more than $1,000 with respect to service performed during any calendar year commencing on or after January 1, 2006;

- **(b)** *Amendment to Internal Revenue Code of 1986* Section 3121(b)(7)(F) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (defining employment) is amended by striking “or” at the end of clause (iv), by adding “or” at the end of clause (v), and by inserting after clause (v) the following new clause:
  > - **(vi)** by any elected or appointed member of the general governing board of any political subdivision of a State if the remuneration paid in a calendar year for such service is not more than $1,000 with respect to service performed during any calendar year commencing on or after January 1, 2006;
