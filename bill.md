# H.R. 5342

> To prohibit certain agency actions regarding the use of certain electronic devices onboard air born commercial airlines.

## § 1. Limitation on agency actions

No Federal Communication Commission, Federal Aviation Administration, or any other agency action may be taken to authorize the use of any electronic device which was banned for use onboard air born commercial airlines by the Federal Aviation Administration on December 15, 2004, until June 1, 2007.
