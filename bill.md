# H.R. 5648

> To amend the Older Americans Act of 1965 to facilitate interaction between students and older individuals with limited English proficiency.

## § 1. Amendment

Section 321(a)(21) is amended by striking “school-age children and older individuals, including” and inserting “students and older individuals, including services to older individuals with limited English proficiency and”.
