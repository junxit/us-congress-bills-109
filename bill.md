# H.R. 1692

> To repeal the application of the sunset in the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 to tuition programs which are qualified under section 529 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Savings for Personal Education Continuation Tax Relief Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Repeal of application of sunset in the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 to 529 plans

Section 901 of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 shall not apply to section 402 of such Act.
