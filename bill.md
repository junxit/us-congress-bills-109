# H.R. 4904

> To amend the Fur Product Labeling Act to require labeling of all fur products, regardless of value.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the “Truth in Fur Labeling Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Fur product labeling

Section 2(d) of the Fur Products Labeling Act (15 U.S.C. 69(d)) is amended by striking “; except that such term” and all that follows through “contained therein” and inserting “, regardless of value, quantity imported, or expense of labeling”.
