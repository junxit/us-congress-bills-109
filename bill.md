# S. 1720

> To provide enhanced penalties for crimes committed using funds appropriated for remediation of any injury or damage caused by Hurricane Katrina.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Katrina Waste, Fraud, and Abuse Deterrence Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Enhanced penalties

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 1 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 26. Enhanced penalties
  >
  > Whoever commits any violation of this title and such violation involves funds appropriated for remediation of any injury or damage caused by Hurricane Katrina shall be fined under this title, imprisoned not less than 5 years and not more than 20 years, or both, in addition to any other penalty authorized.
