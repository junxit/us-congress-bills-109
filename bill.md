# H.R. 5435

> To amend the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 to extend by one year the deadline for the implementation of the Western Hemisphere Travel Initiative.

## § 1. Extension of deadline for implementation of Western Hemisphere Travel Initiative

The second sentence of subsection (b)(1) of section 7209 of the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 (Public Law 108–458) (commonly referred to as the “Western Hemisphere Travel Initiative”) is amended by striking “January 1, 2008,” and inserting “June 1, 2009,”.
