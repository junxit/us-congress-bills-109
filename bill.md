# H. RES. 933

> Commending agents, attorneys, and employees of the Office of the Inspector General of the Department of Justice.

## Preamble

- Whereas the Office of the Inspector General of the Department of Justice was established in 1988 to investigate Department abuse, prevent fraud, and review legislation;
- Whereas the Office of the Inspector General, in its short history, has been instrumental in uncovering waste, fraud, and abuse at the Department;
- Whereas the Office of the Inspector General has exposed abuses of power in the most trying of times; and
- Whereas the agents, attorneys, and employees of the Office of the Inspector General are public servants dedicated to the mission of the Office: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives commends the agents, attorneys, and employees of the Office of the Inspector General of the Department of Justice for their service to their country and pursuit of justice.
