# S. 3936

> To invest in innovation and education to improve the competitiveness of the United States in the global economy.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Competitiveness Investment Act”.

## § 2. Organization of Act into divisions; table of contents

- **(a)** *Divisions* This Act is organized into 4 divisions as follows:
  - **(1)** *Division A* Commerce and Science.
  - **(2)** *Division B* Department of Energy.
  - **(3)** *Division C* Education.
  - **(4)** *Division D* National Science Foundation.
