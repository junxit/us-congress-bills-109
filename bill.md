# S. 168

> To reauthorize additional contract authority for States with Indian reservations.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Indian School Bus Route Safety Reauthorization Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of additional contract authority for States with Indian reservations

Section 1214(d)(5)(A) of the Transportation Equity Act for the 21st Century (23 U.S.C. 202 note; 112 Stat. 206) is amended by striking “$1,500,000 for each of fiscal years 1998 through 2003” and inserting “$1,800,000 for each of fiscal years 2005 through 2010”.
