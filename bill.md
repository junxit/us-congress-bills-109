# S. 48

> To reauthorize appropriations for the New Jersey Coastal Heritage Trail Route, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Reauthorization of Appropriations for New Jersey Coastal Heritage Trail Route

- **(a)** *Reauthorization* Section 6 of Public Law 100–515 (16 U.S.C. 1244 note) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (b)(1), by striking “$4,000,000” and all that follows and inserting “such sums as are necessary”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (c), by striking “10” and inserting “12”.
- **(b)** *Strategic plan*
  - **(1)** *In general* Not later than 2 years after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Interior shall prepare a strategic plan for the New Jersey Coastal Heritage Trail Route.
  - **(2)** *Contents* The strategic plan shall describe—
    - **(A)** opportunities to increase participation by national and local private and public interests in the planning, development, and administration of the New Jersey Coastal Heritage Trail Route; and
    - **(B)** organizational options for sustaining the New Jersey Coastal Heritage Trail Route.
