# S. 3803

> To establish national and State putative father registries, to make grants to States to promote responsible fatherhood, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Rights of Unknowing Dads and Fostering Access To Help Encourage Responsibility (Proud Father) Act of 2006”.
