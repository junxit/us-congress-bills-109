# S. 2338

> To extend the authority of the Secretary of the Army to accept and expend funds contributed by non-Federal public entities to expedite the processing of permits.

## § 1. Funding to process permits

Section 214(c) of the Water Resources Development Act of 2000 (Public Law 106–541; 33 U.S.C. 2201 note) is amended by striking “March 31, 2006” and inserting “December 31, 2006”.
