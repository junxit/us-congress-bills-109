# S. 3362

> To exempt woven fiberglass mesh fabric from certain quotas.

## § 1. Woven fiberglass mesh fabric

- **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the quota imposed under category 622 established pursuant to the Committee for the Implementation of Textile Agreements (CITA) with respect to certain textile and apparel articles shall not apply to woven fiberglass mesh fabric provided for in subheading 7019.40.40 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States.
- **(b)** *Effective Date* The provision in subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
