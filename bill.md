# H.R. 2442

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on Thionyl Chloride.

## § 1. Thionyl Chloride

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.28.01 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Thionyl Chloride) is amended by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2009”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
