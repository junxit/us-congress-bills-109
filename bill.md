# S. 2596

> To modify the boundaries for a certain empowerment zone designation.

## § 1. Modification of boundary of Aroostook County empowerment zone

- **(a)** *In general* The Aroostook County empowerment zone shall include, in addition to the area designated as of the date of the enactment of this Act, the remaining area of the county not included in such designation, notwithstanding the size requirement of section 1392(a)(3)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 and the population requirements of section 1392(a)(1)(B) of such Code.
- **(b)** *Effective date* Subsection (a) shall take effect as of the effective date of the designation of the Aroostook County empowerment zone by the Secretary of Agriculture.
