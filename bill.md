# H.R. 2806

> To reduce temporarily the duty on Paraquat Dichloride.

## § 1. Paraquat Dichloride

- **(a)** *Calendar year 2006*
  - **(1)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by inserting in numerical sequence the following new heading:
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendment made by paragraph (1) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Calendar year 2007*
  - **(1)** *In general* Heading 9902.09.12 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, as added by subsection (a), is amended—
    - **(A)** by striking “3.59%” and inserting “4.02%”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “On or before 12/31/2006” and inserting “On or before 12/31/2007”.
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendments made by paragraph (1) shall take effect on January 1, 2007.
- **(c)** *Calendar year 2008*
  - **(1)** *In general* Heading 9902.09.11 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, as added by subsection (a) and amended by this section, is further amended—
    - **(A)** by striking “4.02%” and inserting “4.41%”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “On or before 12/31/2007” and inserting “On or before 12/31/2008”.
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendments made by paragraph (1) shall take effect on January 1, 2008.
