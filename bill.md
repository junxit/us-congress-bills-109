# S. 442

> To provide for the Secretary of Homeland Security to be included in the line of Presidential succession.

## § 1. Secretary of Homeland Security in Presidential line of succession

Section 19(d)(1) of title 3, United States Code, is amended by inserting “Secretary of Homeland Security,” after “Attorney General,”.
