# H.R. 5591

> To name the national cemetery being constructed in Dixon, California, as the “Solano National Cemetery”.

## § 1. Name of national cemetery, Dixon, California

The national cemetery being constructed by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs in Dixon, California, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Solano National Cemetery”. Any reference to such cemetery in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the Solano National Cemetery.
