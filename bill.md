# H. CON. RES. 11

> Requiring the display of the Ten Commandments in the Hall of the House of Representatives and the Chamber of the Senate.

That a copy of the Ten Commandments shall be prominently displayed in the Hall of the House of Representatives and the Chamber of the Senate.
