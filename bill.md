# S. 2301

> To suspend temporarily the duty on synthetic quartz or synthetic fused silica.

## § 1. Temporary suspension of duty on substrates of synthetic quartz or synthetic fused silica

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by striking heading 9902.70.06 and inserting the following:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
