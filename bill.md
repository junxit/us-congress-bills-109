# H.R. 5882

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to increase the minimum Federal medical assistance percentage under the Medicaid Program for States to 53 percent.

## § 1. Increase in minimum FMAP for States to 53 percent

- **(a)** *In general* Clause (1) of section 1905(b) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396d(b)) is amended by inserting “(or, only for purposes of this title in the case of any of the 50 States, 53 percent)” after “50 per centum”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to calendar quarters beginning on or after October 1, 2007.
