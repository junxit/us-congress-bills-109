# H.R. 1963

> To reduce temporarily the duty on Mesotrione Technical.

## § 1. Mesotrione Technical

- **(a)** *Calendar year 2006*
  - **(1)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by inserting in numerical sequence the following:
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendment made by paragraph (1) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Calendar year 2007*
  - **(1)** *In general* Heading 9902.07.01, as added by subsection (a), is amended—
    - **(A)** by striking “6.04%” and inserting “6.08%”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2007”.
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendments made by paragraph (1) shall take effect on January 1, 2007.
- **(c)** *Calendar year 2008*
  - **(1)** *In general* Heading 9902.07.01, as added by subsection (a) and amended by subsection (b), is further amended—
    - **(A)** by striking “6.08%” and inserting “6.11%”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “12/31/2007” and inserting “12/31/2008”.
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendments made by paragraph (1) shall take effect on January 1, 2008.
