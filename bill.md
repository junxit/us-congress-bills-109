# H.R. 6324

> To provide a new effective date for the applicability of certain provisions of law to Public Law 105–331.

That, notwithstanding subparagraph (B) of section 5134(f)(1) of title 31, United States Code, the entity described in section 7(b)(7) of Public Law 105–331 shall have until June 30, 2007, to satisfy the requirement of subparagraph (A)(ii) of such section 5134(f)(1).
