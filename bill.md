# H.R. 6320

> To create an additional judgeship for the eastern district of California, and for other purposes.

## § 1. District judgeship for the eastern district of California

- **(a)** *Additional permanent district judgeship* The President shall appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, 1 additional district judge for the eastern district of California.
- **(b)** *Technical and conforming amendment* In order that the table under section 133(a) of title 28, United States Code, will reflect the change in the number of permanent judgeships authorized for the eastern district of California by subsection (a), such table is amended in the item relating to the eastern district of California by striking “6” and inserting “7”.
