# S. 1769

> To provide relief to individuals and businesses affected by Hurricane Katrina related to healthcare and health insurance coverage, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Public Health and Health Insurance Emergency Response Act of 2005”.
