# H.R. 4442

> To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to reestablish the hazard mitigation program cap at 15 percent of major disaster assistance, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Hazard mitigation

- **(a)** *In general* Section 404(a) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5170c(a)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “up to 75 percent” and inserting “up to 90 percent”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “not exceed 7.5” and inserting “not exceed 15”.
- **(b)** *Applicability* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to a major disaster declared by the President after August 1, 2005.
