# H.R. 5174

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to prevent veterans’ contributions to education benefits from reducing Federal student financial assistance.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veterans’ Equity in Education Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Veterans' benefits

Section 480(j) (20 U.S.C. 1087vv(j)) is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(4)** Notwithstanding paragraph (1), for every year a student receives veterans' education benefits under chapter 30 of title 38, United States Code, the amount of such veterans’ education benefits that the student receives and that is treated as estimated financial assistance shall be reduced by the amount that the student’s basic pay was reduced under section 3011(b) or 3012(c) of such title in order to be eligible to receive such benefits.
