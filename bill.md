# H.R. 1231

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to restore and make permanent the exclusion from gross income for amounts received under qualified group legal services plans and to repeal the limitation on the amount of the exclusion.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Legal Benefits Tax Fairness Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Exclusion for amounts received under qualified group legal services plans restored and made permanent

- **(a)** *In general* Section 120 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking subsection (e).
- **(b)** *Repeal of limitation on amount of exclusion* Subsection (a) of section 120 of such Code is amended by striking the last sentence.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2004.
