# H.R. 1768

> To amend the provision of law establishing the Presidential 9/11 Heroes Medals of Valor to make certain technical corrections to carry out the intent of the provision.

That subsection (d) of section 124 (31 U.S.C. 5111 note) of division B of Public Law 108–447 is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2), by striking “with the Secretary of the Treasury for the Secretary to prepare and strike, on a reimbursable basis,” and inserting “for striking”; and
- **(2)** by striking paragraph (3).
