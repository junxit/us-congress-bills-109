# S. CON. RES. 80

> Relating to the enrollment of S. 1932.

That the enrollment of the bill S. 1932 as presented to the President for his signature on February 8, 2006, is deemed the true enrollment of the bill reflecting the intent of the Congress in enacting the bill into law.
