# H.R. 2180

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide for the proper tax treatment of variable contracts issued by life insurance companies subject to the laws of Puerto Rico.

## § 1. Tax treatment of variable contracts issued by life insurance companies subject to the laws of Puerto Rico

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 817(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to variable contract defined) is amended by striking “pursuant to State law or regulation” and inserting “pursuant to the law or regulation of a State or Puerto Rico”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to contracts issued after the date of the enactment of this Act.
