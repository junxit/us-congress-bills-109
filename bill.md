# S. 1928

> To reduce mandatory and discretionary spending in order to offset the cost of rebuilding the Gulf Region in the wake of Hurricane Katrina and Hurricane Rita.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Spending Money Accountably to Rebuild After Tragedy Act” or the “SMART Act”.
