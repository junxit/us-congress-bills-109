# H.R. 4677

> To impose a two year moratorium on the approval by the Secretary of the Interior of new Tribal-State compacts for gaming under the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act.

## § 1. Two year moratorium on new Indian casinos

The Secretary of the Interior shall not approve any new Tribal-State compacts for gaming under the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act (25 U.S.C. 2701 et seq.) until after the date that is 2 years after the date of the enactment of this Act.
