# H. RES. 1020

> Directing the Secretary of Defense to provide certain information to the House of Representatives relating to Maher Arar.

That the Secretary of Defense is directed to provide to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, all documents and records in the possession of the Secretary relating to Maher Arar.
