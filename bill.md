# S. 2306

> To amend the National Organ Transplant Act to clarify that kidney paired donation and kidney list donation do not involve the transfer of a human organ for valuable consideration.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Living Kidney Organ Donation Clarification Act”.

## § 2. Amendment to the National Organ Transplant Act

Section 301(a) of the National Organ Transplant Act (42 U.S.C. 274e(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following: “For purposes of this section, kidney paired donation and kidney list donation shall not be considered to involve the transfer of a human organ for valuable consideration.”.
