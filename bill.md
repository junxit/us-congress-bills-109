> To designate certain land in New England as wilderness for inclusion in the National Wilderness Preservation system and certain land as a National Recreation Area, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

## § 2. Definition of Secretary

In this Act, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Agriculture, acting through the Chief of the Forest Service.
