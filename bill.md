# S. 1825

> To amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 and the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to revise the funding and deduction rules for multiemployer defined benefit plans, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Multiemployer Plan Funding and Deduction Reform Act of 2005”.
