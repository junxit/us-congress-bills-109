# H.R. 2303

> To extend the suspension of duty on certain light absorbing photo dyes.

## § 1. Certain light absorbing photo dyes

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.29.34 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain light absorbing photo dyes) is amended by striking the date in the effetive period column and inserting “12/31/2010”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
