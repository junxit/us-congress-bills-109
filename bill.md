# H.R. 5426

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 326 South Main Street in Princeton, Illinois, as the “Congressman Owen Lovejoy Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Congressman Owen Lovejoy Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 326 South Main Street in Princeton, Illinois, shall be known and designated as the “Congressman Owen Lovejoy Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Congressman Owen Lovejoy Post Office Building”.
