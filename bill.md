# H.R. 3262

> To modify the civil money penalties incurred for unlawful employment of aliens.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Employee Verification Accountability Act”.

## § 2. Civil money penalties for unlawful employment of aliens

Section 274A(e)(4)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1324a(e)(4)(A)) is amended to read as follows:
