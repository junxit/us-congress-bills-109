# S. 1124

> To postpone by 1 year the date by which countries participating in the visa waiver program shall begin to issue machine-readable tamper-resistant entry passports.

## § 1. Extension of deadline for the issuance of machine-readable, tamper-resistant passports

Section 303 of the Enhanced Border Security and Visa Entry Reform Act of 2002 (8 U.S.C. 1732) is amended by striking “2005” each place it appears and inserting “2006”.
