# H. RES. 924

> Providing for consideration of the bill (S. 2754) to derive human pluripotent stem cell lines using techniques that do not knowingly harm embryos.

That upon the adoption of this resolution it shall be in order without intervention of any point of order to consider in the House the bill (S. 2754) to derive human pluripotent stem cell lines using techniques that do not knowingly harm embryos. The bill shall be considered as read. The previous question shall be considered as ordered on the bill to final passage without intervening motion except: (1) one hour of debate equally divided and controlled by the chairman and ranking minority member of the Committee on Energy and Commerce; and (2) one motion to recommit.
