# H.R. 3623

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to increase to 5 years the period during which former Members of Congress may not engage in certain lobbying activities.

## § 1. Increase to 5 years of period during which former Members of Congress may not engage in certain lobbying activities

Section 207(e)(1)(A) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking “1 year” and inserting “5 years”.
