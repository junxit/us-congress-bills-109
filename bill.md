# H.R. 2129

> To improve the coordination of programs for the Great Lakes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as “Great Lakes Environmental Protection and Restoration Programs Reauthorization Act” .

## § 2. Great Lakes defined

For purposes of this Act, the term “Great Lakes” means Lake Ontario, Lake Erie, Lake Huron (including Lake St. Clair), Lake Michigan, and Lake Superior, and the connecting channels (Saint Mary’s River, Saint Clair River, Detroit River, Niagara River, and Saint Lawrence River to the Canadian Border).
