# H.R. 3209

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to add nasopharyngeal cancer to the statutorily prescribed presumptive diseases associated with exposure to Agent Orange during military service in Vietnam.

## § 1. Addition of nasopharyngeal cancer to the statutorily prescribed presumptive diseases associated with exposure to Agent Orange during military service in Vietnam

Section 1116(a)(2)(F) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting “or nasopharyngeal cancer” after “or trachea)”.
