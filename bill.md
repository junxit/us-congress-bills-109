# S. 3964

> To provide for the issuance of a commemorative postage stamp in honor of Senator Blanche Kelso Bruce.

## § 1. Senator Blanche Kelso Bruce commemorative postage stamp

- **(a)** *In general* The Postmaster General shall issue a commemorative postage stamp in honor of Senator Blanche Kelso Bruce.
- **(b)** *Denomination and design* The commemorative postage stamp issued under this Act shall—
  - **(1)** be issued in the denomination used for 1st class mail up to 1 ounce in weight; and
  - **(2)** bear such designs as the Postmaster General shall determine.
- **(c)** *Issuance period* The commemorative postage stamp issued under this Act shall—
  - **(1)** be placed on sale as soon as practicable after the date of the enactment of this Act; and
  - **(2)** be sold for such period thereafter as the Postmaster General shall determine.
