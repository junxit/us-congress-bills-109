# S. RES. 164

> Authorizing the printing with illustrations of a document entitled “Committee on Appropriations, United States Senate, 138th Anniversary, 1867–2005”.

That there be printed with illustrations as a Senate document a compilation of materials entitled “Committee on Appropriations, United States Senate, 138th Anniversary, 1867–2005”, and that there be printed two thousand additional copies of such document for the use of the Committee on Appropriations.
