# H.R. 268

> To repeal the sunset of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 with respect to the expansion of the adoption credit and adoption assistance programs.

## § 1. Repeal of applicability of sunset of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 with respect to adoption credit and adoption assistance programs

Title IX of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 shall not apply to the amendments made by section 202 of such Act (relating to expansion of adoption credit and adoption assistance programs).
