# H.R. 5225

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to prevent and cure diabetes and to promote and improve the care of individuals with diabetes for the reduction of health disparities within racial and ethnic minority groups, including the African-American, Hispanic American, Asian American and Pacific Islander, and American Indian and Alaskan Native communities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Diabetes Prevention Access and Care Act”.
