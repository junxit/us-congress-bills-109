# H.R. 1255

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the tax incentives for biodiesel.

## § 1. Extension of biodiesel tax credits

- **(a)** *In general* Sections 40A(e), 6426(c)(6), and 6427(e)(3)(B) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 are each amended by striking “2006” and inserting “2010”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.
