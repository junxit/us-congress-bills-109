# H. CON. RES. 480

> To correct the enrollment of the bill H.R. 3127.

That, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 3127) entitled “An Act to impose sanctions against individuals responsible for genocide, war crimes, and crimes against humanity, to support measures for the protection of civilians and humanitarian operations, and to support peace efforts in the Darfur region of Sudan, and for other purposes”, the Clerk of the House of Representatives is hereby authorized and directed to make the following corrections in section 8(c)(1) of the bill:

- **(1)** Strike “to support” and insert “and to support”.
- **(2)** Strike “and to implement” and insert “or to implement”.
