# S. 2425

> To apply amendments to the Immigration and Nationality Act related to providing medical services in underserved areas, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Application of Certain Amendments to All Aliens

Section 220(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Technical Corrections Act of 1994 (8 U.S.C. 1182 note; Public Law 103–416) (as amended by section 1(a)(1) of Public Law 108–441) is amended by striking “Act and before June 1, 2006.” and inserting “Act.”.
