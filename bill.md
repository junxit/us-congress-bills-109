# H.R. 1364

> To amend title 28, United States Code, to enable the Supreme Court to review decisions in which the Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces denied relief.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Equal Justice for Our Military Act”.

## § 2. Supreme Court review of decisions in which the Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces denied relief

Section 1259 of title 28, United States Code, is amended in paragraph (4) by striking “granted relief” and inserting “granted or denied relief”.
