# S. 1985

> To extend the predisaster hazard mitigation program under the Stafford Act.

## § 1. Predisaster hazard mitigation program

Section 203(m) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5133(m)) is amended by striking “December 31, 2005” and inserting “September 30, 2007”.
