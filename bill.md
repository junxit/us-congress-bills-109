# H.R. 1285

> To amend the Nursing Relief for Disadvantaged Areas Act of 1999 to remove the limitation for nonimmigrant classification for nurses in health professional shortage areas.

## § 1. Removal of limitation for nonimmigrant classification for nurses in health professional shortage areas

Section 2(e) of the Nursing Relief for Disadvantaged Areas Act of 1999 (Public Law 106–95; 113 Stat. 1317) is repealed.
