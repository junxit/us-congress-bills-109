# H.R. 4111

> To redesignate the Caribbean National Forest in the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico as the El Yunque National Forest.

## § 1. El Yunque National Forest

- **(a)** *Redesignation* The Caribbean National Forest in the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico is hereby redesignated and shall be known as the “El Yunque National Forest”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the National Forest in the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “El Yunque National Forest”.
