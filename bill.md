# S. 2625

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on 1-octadecanaminium, N,N-di-methyl-N-octadecyl-, (SP-4-2)-[29H,31H-phtha-locyanine-2-sulfonato(3-)-kN29,kN30,kN31,kN32]cuprate(1-).

## § 1. 1-octadecanaminium, N,N-di-methyl-N-octadecyl-, (SP-4-2)-[29H,31H-phtha-locyanine-2-sulfonato(3-)-kN29,kN30,kN31,kN32]cuprate(1-)

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.03.26 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 1-octadecanaminium, N,N-di-methyl-N-octadecyl-, (SP-4-2)-[29H,31H-phtha-locyanine-2-sulfonato(3-)-kN29,kN30,kN31,kN32]cuprate(1-)) is amended by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2009”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
