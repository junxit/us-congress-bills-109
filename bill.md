# H.R. 2524

> To extend the suspension of duty on Bis(4-fluorophenyl) methanone.

## § 1. Bis(4-fluorophenyl) methanone

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.32.85 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Bis(4-fluorophenyl) methanone) is amended by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2010”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
