# H. RES. 846

> Requesting the President and directing the Secretary of State to provide to the House of Representatives certain documents in their possession relating to strategies and plans either designed to cause regime change in or for the use of military force against Iran.

That the President is requested and the Secretary of State is directed to provide to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, all documents, including planning documents, electronic mail records, minutes, memoranda, and advisory legal opinions, in the possession of the President or the Secretary of State, respectively, relating to strategies, and plans either designed to cause regime change in or for the use of military force against Iran.
