# H.R. 605

> To authorize the President to award the Medal of Honor posthumously to Garlin Murl Conner for acts of valor during World War II.

## § 1. Authority for award of Medal of Honor to Garlin Murl Conner

Notwithstanding the time limitations specified in section 3744(b) of title 10, United States Code, or any other time limitation, the President may award the Medal of Honor posthumously to Garlin Murl Conner of Clinton County, Kentucky, for acts of valor during World War II while serving in the 3d Infantry Division of the United States Army.
