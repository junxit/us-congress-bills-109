# S. 2800

> To designate the Department of Veterans Affairs Outpatient Clinic in Tulsa, Oklahoma, as the “Ernest Childers Department of Veterans Affairs Outpatient Clinic”.

## § 1. Ernest Childers Department of Veterans Affairs Outpatient Clinic

- **(a)** *Designation* The Department of Veterans Affairs Outpatient Clinic in Tulsa, Oklahoma, shall be known and designated as the “Ernest Childers Department of Veterans Affairs Outpatient Clinic”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States to the outpatient clinic referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Ernest Childers Department of Veterans Affairs Outpatient Clinic.
