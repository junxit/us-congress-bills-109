# S. RES. 616

> Authorizing the Majority Leader and one staff member to travel to Mexico for the inauguration of the new President of Mexico scheduled for December 2, 2006.

That the Majority Leader and one staff member are authorized to travel to Mexico for the inauguration of the new President of Mexico scheduled for December 2, 2006.
