# H.R. 1864

> To provide for enhanced retirement benefits for administrative law judges.

## § 1. Short title; references

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Administrative Law Judges Retirement Act of 2005”.
- **(b)** *References* Whenever in this Act an amendment is expressed in terms of an amendment to a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of title 5, United States Code.

## § 4. Effective date; applicability

- **(a)** *Effective date* This Act and the amendments made by this Act shall take effect as of the first day of the first pay period beginning on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Applicability* Nothing in this Act shall be considered to apply with respect to any annuity entitlement to which is based on a separation from service occurring before the effective date of this Act.
