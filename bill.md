# H.R. 2205

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to extend the veterans’ preference provisions of such title to individuals who served on active duty in the armed forces for a period of more than 180 consecutive days any part of which occurred after September 11, 2001, and before January 1, 2006, and separated from the armed forces under honorable conditions.

## § 1. Veterans’ preference

Section 2108(1) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (C), by adding “or” after the semicolon; and
- **(3)** by inserting after subparagraph (C) the following:
  > - **(D)** served on active duty as defined by section 101(21) of title 38 at any time in the armed forces for a period of more than 180 consecutive days any part of which occurred after September 11, 2001, and before January 1, 2006;
