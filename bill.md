# S. CON. RES. 73

> Urging the President to issue a proclamation for the observance of an American Jewish History Month.

That Congress urges the President to issue each year a proclamation calling on State and local governments and the people of the United States to observe an American Jewish History Month with appropriate programs, ceremonies, and activities.
