# H.R. 3555

> To authorize the Secretary of Health and Human Services to award on a competitive basis grants to public and private entities to establish or expand teenage pregnancy prevention programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Responsible Funding to Prevent Teen Pregnancy Act”.
