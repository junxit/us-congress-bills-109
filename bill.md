# H.R. 3721

> To amend the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 to allow certain commercial vehicles to continue to use Route 209 within Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area and to allow the National Park Service to continue to collect fees from those vehicles, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Use of certain roads within Delaware Water Gap

Section 702 of Division I of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–333; 110 Stat. 4185) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “2005” and inserting “2015” each place it appears;
- **(2)** in subsection (c)(2), by striking “$25” and inserting “$40”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following new subsection:
  > - **(d)** *River Road* The Secretary of the Interior shall ensure that any proposal that is adopted by the National Park Service to maintain or reconstruct River Road within Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area provides for that road to be open to two-way traffic. This subsection shall not prohibit the Secretary from allowing temporary closures of one or more lanes of traffic on River Road for emergency or public safety purposes.
