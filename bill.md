# H.R. 1511

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide a 5-year extension of the credit for electricity produced from wind.

## § 1. 5-Year extension of credit for electricity produced from wind

Section 45(d)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to wind facility) is amended by striking “2006” and inserting “2011”.
