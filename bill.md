# H.R. 4715

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to extend the 2006 annual coordinated election period for Medicare prescription drug plans through August 15, 2006.

## § 1. Three-month extension of the 2006 open enrollment period for Medicare prescription drug plans

Section 1860D–1(b)(1)(B)(iii) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–101(b)(1)(B)(iii)) is amended by inserting “, except that in applying paragraph (1) of such section, with respect to the annual coordinated election period for 2006, such period shall continue through August 15, 2006” before the period.
