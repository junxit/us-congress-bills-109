# H.R. 3165

> To amend the Occupational Safety and Health Act to provide for criminal liability for willful safety standard violations resulting in the death of contract employees.

## § 1. Definition of Employee

Section 17(e) of the Occupational Safety and Health Act (29 U.S.C. 666(e)) is amended by inserting “including contract employees who are contracted by the employer,” after “employee,”.
