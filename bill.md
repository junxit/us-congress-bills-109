# H.R. 5628

> To provide for an initial period of admission of 36 months for aliens employed as dairy workers.

## § 1. Period of admission for aliens employed as dairy workers

An alien admitted under section 101(a)(15)(H)(ii)(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(15)(H)(ii)(a)) for employment as a dairy worker may be admitted for an initial period of 36 months.
