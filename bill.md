# H.R. 4794

> To repeal a provision enacted to end Federal matching of State spending of child support incentive payments.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Child Support Protection Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Repeal of provision enacted to end Federal matching of State spending of child support incentive payments

Section 7309 of the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005 is repealed.
