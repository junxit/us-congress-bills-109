# H. CON. RES. 43

> Expressing the sense of the Congress that Social Security reform measures should not force State and local government employees into Social Security coverage.

## Preamble

- Whereas an estimated 5 million State and local government employees are currently covered under public pensions outside the Social Security program; and
- Whereas these State and local government employees deserve to be secure in the knowledge that their coverage will not be affected against their will by Social Security reform measures: Now, therefore, be it

That no Social Security reform plan favorably considered by the Congress should cause State and local government employees covered under public pensions to be forced into coverage under the old-age, survivors, and disability insurance program under title II of the Social Security Act.
