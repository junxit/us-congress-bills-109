# S. 3836

> To reauthorize the United States Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “United States Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy Reauthorization Act of 2006”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of United States Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy

Section 1334 of the Foreign Affairs Reform and Restructuring Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 6553), as amended by section 410 of the Department of State and Related Agency Appropriations Act, 2006 (Public Law 109–108; 119 Stat. 2327), is amended by striking “October 1, 2006” and inserting “October 1, 2009”.
