# H.R. 2587

> To make amendments to the Reclamation Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992.

## § 1. Reclamation Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act amendments

Section 1631 of the Reclamation Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992, as amended by Public Law 104–266 (43 U.S.C. 390h–13), is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a), by inserting before the period the following: “, including Phase II of the Olivenhain Water Treatment Plant”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (b)(2), by adding at the end the following: “Notwithstanding any other provision of this title, the Federal share of the costs of the projects for which funds are made available under this section shall not exceed $35,000,000.”.
