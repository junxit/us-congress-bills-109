# S. 1230

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide for the extension of the Highway Trust Fund and the Aquatic Resources Trust Fund expenditure authority and related taxes and to provide for excise tax reform and simplification, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; amendment of 1986 Code; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Highway Reauthorization and Excise Tax Simplification Act of 2005”.
- **(b)** *Amendment of 1986 Code* Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
