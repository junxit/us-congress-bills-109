# H.R. 2913

> To suspend temporarily the duty on Thiamethoxam Technical.

## § 1. Thiamethoxam Technical

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.03.11 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Thiamethoxam Technical) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “2.54%” and inserting “Free”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2008”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
