# S. 1339

> To reauthorize the Junior Duck Stamp Conservation and Design Program Act of 1994.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Junior Duck Stamp Reauthorization Act of 2005”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

The Junior Duck Stamp Conservation and Design Program Act of 1994 (16 U.S.C. 719 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating the first section 6 (16 U.S.C. 719c), relating to authorization of appropriations, as section 7 and moving the section so as to appear at the end of the Act; and
- **(2)** in section 7 (as redesignated by paragraph (1)) by striking “2001 through 2005” and inserting “2006 through 2010”.
