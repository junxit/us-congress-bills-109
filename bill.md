# H.R. 4930

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to clarify that State and local permitting requirements relating to the processing, sorting, or transporting of solid waste apply to rail carriers.

## § 1. Applicability of permitting requirements to rail carriers

Section 10501 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(d)** Nothing in this part shall be construed to exempt a rail carrier from State or local permitting requirements relating to the processing, sorting, or transporting of solid waste.
